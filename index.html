How to play

Read the schedule. Risk the town.

Before each bell, declare a prediction about the scheduled event. A correct call earns Momentum; spend 1 Momentum to skip a district scene you mastered in an earlier loop. Every action adds 1 Fracture, while dangerous actions add 2. At 4, catastrophe advances and midnight arrives early. At the Anchor Board, choose one bond and one object to survive the reset. Route, song, lens, and every Scar persist automatically.
