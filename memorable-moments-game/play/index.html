A SMALL INVITATION

Two people.
One ordinary moment.

Pick a scene. Take turns choosing and guessing in private. Reveal together, make something small away from the screen, then each add one detail to your keepsake.

About five minutes. No score, streak, account, camera, or need to share anything personal. You can imagine the objects, change the scene, or leave whenever you like.

A curtain is a courtesy, not a security lock. Ask the other person to look away. Anyone with this device or browser tools can inspect the session. Answers stay in this tab’s memory; reloading clears them. Only an explicit download saves your keepsake.

Friends, partners, family: bring someone who wants to join. Solo rehearsal shows the flow, but cannot test a shared experience.
