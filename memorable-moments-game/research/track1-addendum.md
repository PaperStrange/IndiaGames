## New convergences — how the addendum moves the synthesis

**1. Audience segmentation: triple-converged → recommend resolving gate #3 toward separate editions.**
Physical decks (TableTopics 13+ editions; BestSelf most granular; Skin Deep stage editions) + anti-pattern evidence (Esther Perel single-box-for-all = thinnest reviews despite biggest name) + digital traction stratification (Marco Polo/BackThen family vs Cozy Couples/Spicer/Desire couples vs Cocoon ~5K weak) all point one way. Proposed revision: default to separate editions (couples / friends / family minimum) on a shared engine — not one game.

**2. Digital monetization norm discovered → narrows finding #7's digital [OPEN].**
Every live digital player runs free core + subscription; ZERO one-time-purchase digital models observed (Marco Polo $9.99/mo·$79.99/yr; BackThen ~$5/mo; Cupla $4.99/mo·$44.99/yr; Lasting $12/mo·$80/yr for two; Spicer Plus + à-la-carte). Norm: one subscription covers the pair/group (Cupla, OurCouple, Lasting). New decision: keep core play FREE — monetizing the play content itself is the #1 review complaint (Desire's coins gating dares; Spicer's paywall complaints). Monetize premium features + keepsake upsell instead. This converges with finding #7's hygiene constraint (one purchase = both partners).

**3. Gate #1 sharpened: digital reveal proven for Q&A matching, [OPEN] for mission commitment.**
Spicer (decade-live mutual Yes/Maybe match), DoYouKnow? clones (guess-partner's-answer = cheapest proven loop), Kindu DEAD (double-blind match died on distribution; intimacy-gating is store-hostile). The Skin Deep tried app-based video save/share and RETREATED to downloads — a content brand failing at app capture/share warns that bolting an app onto content IP fails; digital-native required. Finding #1 holds with finer grain; Spicer's 24h challenge window is the closest digital stake mechanic observed — partial evidence for the "stake" half of the gap.

**4. Family/friends async capture works at scale — but it's messaging, not moment-manufacturing.**
Marco Polo 29M installs proves async family video capture sustains a business; Cocoon ~5K proves private-sharing alone doesn't grow. The manufacture + capture + share combination remains unoccupied across all 15 new competitors. Strengthens finding #8's "archives don't retain without a loop."

**5. BackThen validates the subscription + keepsake-upsell hybrid** (VIP sub + printed Photo Books, 4.85★, updated 2026) — the template for create → capture → share/print. Strengthens finding #8's keepsake direction (attach rate still [OPEN]).

**6. New portable mechanics approved:** escalating vulnerability (Actually Curious 4 levels; WNRS 3 levels; Skin Deep stage editions) as the best-studied depth pattern; depth MUST be calibratable (BestSelf verified review: deck "can go too deep" — wife cried [REAL]); "ritual, not game night" framing (BestSelf weekly) approves repeated-session design; Spicer's time-boxed challenge as stake precedent; SFW-broad (Kindu store-removal warning).

## E. Game platforms beyond mobile (Steam / itch.io / web party / co-op) — 2026-10-03

**Why this sweep:** platform breadth beyond mobile app stores, per 2026-10-03 direction. Same evidence discipline: [REAL] = verified this run, [SECONDARY] = cited published source, [OPEN] = unvalidated. No design, no PRD.

### E1. Jackbox Party Packs — the category king of manufactured game-night moments
- (1) PC/console/TV + phones-as-controllers party packs: 5 games per annual pack, 2–8 active players + audience; everyone joins via room code at jackbox.tv on their phone — **no app download required** (CEO Mike Bilder: ~a year of server engineering to avoid app downloads) [SECONDARY — Wikipedia].
- (2) 100M players (2019) → **200M (Oct 2020, company statement)** [SECONDARY]; sales +1,000% Mar–May 2020 [SECONDARY]; 11 packs 2014–2025, Pack 12 due Oct 2026 [SECONDARY]; Partyverse ad-supported subscription launching 2026 [SECONDARY].
- (3) WHY memorable: **the players' own answers/drawings ARE the content** (Fibbage lies, Quiplash jokes, Drawful drawings) — every round is unrepeatable because the group is the content; simultaneous reveal + group voting manufactures the peak (finding #2).
- (4) ~$25–30 per pack, one-time; one owner hosts, everyone else plays free on their phone [SECONDARY].
- → DECISION: APPROVES **player-generated content as the moment engine** — the strongest observed answer to gate #4 (content supply past month 12): the group authors infinite content for free. APPROVES the **audience mechanic as the passive-participation template** (late joiners become audience, vote, and swing scoring without performing — directly relevant to the #3/#6 collision). APPROVES **zero-install onboarding** as a design variable. Family-friendly filter option noted [SECONDARY].

### E2. Hazelight: It Takes Two / A Way Out / Split Fiction — designed-for-two is a mass market
- (1) Mandatory 2-player co-op adventures (PC/console) — no single-player mode exists; the relationship IS the game.
- (2) **50M cumulative (Apr 2026, studio announcement): It Takes Two 30M, A Way Out 13M, Split Fiction 7M** (1M in 48h, 3 world records for local co-op) [SECONDARY — press]; It Takes Two: Game of the Year 2021 [SECONDARY].
- (3) WHY memorable: mechanics never repeat (each level invents a new co-op verb) + a shared narrative about the relationship itself (a divorcing couple; two writers); split-screen keeps both players' reactions visible (finding #3).
- (4) Premium one-time (~$40); **Friend's Pass: one copy = two players**, the second plays free [SECONDARY].
- → DECISION: PROVES the couples segment is a **mass market, not a niche** — the strongest possible support for the gate-#3 resolution (separate couples edition). Friend's Pass is **"one purchase covers the pair" at AAA scale** — converges with finding #7's hygiene constraint. Constantly-novel mechanics = the anti-exhaustion template (gate #4).

### E3. Forced-communication co-op: Keep Talking and Nobody Explodes / We Were Here
- **Keep Talking:** (1) asymmetric party — one player sees the bomb, the others hold the (printable!) manual; (2) **200K+ copies as of 2016** (GDC announcement; current total unverified) [SECONDARY]; (3) WHY: information asymmetry FORCES precise real-time communication under a timer — the memory is the miscommunication ("cut the RED wire!"); (4) premium one-time across VR/PC/console/mobile.
- **We Were Here series:** (1) 2-player walkie-talkie puzzle co-op, cross-platform; (2) **20M+ players series-wide; first game free (~10M downloads); Expeditions: The FriendShip 8M+ downloads in year one** [SECONDARY — dev announcement]; 90%+ Steam positivity [SECONDARY]; (3) WHY: separated players with different information must narrate their world to each other — **the retelling IS the gameplay** (finding #5, literalized); (4) free pilot → paid sequels funnel.
- → DECISION: the **purest digital proof of finding #3** (synchronous joint action bonds). APPROVES **asymmetric roles as the passive-partner-tolerant template** (one drives, one advises — both perform, neither authors alone). Keep Talking's printable manual = physical-digital hybrid precedent. We Were Here's free-first funnel = proven co-op acquisition.

### E4. Among Us — emergent social stories at planetary scale
- (1) 4–15 player social deduction (mobile/PC/console); (2) **1 BILLION Google Play downloads (Apr 2026, Innersloth announcement)** [SECONDARY]; 3.2M Switch copies in first month [SECONDARY — SuperData]; 500M MAU at 2020 peak [SECONDARY]; (3) WHY: **zero authored story** — the emergency-meeting debate manufactures the memory; accusation, bluffing, betrayal = arousal either valence (finding #2); the discussion/vote phase is a **built-in retelling ritual** (finding #5); "sus" entered the lexicon; (4) free on mobile, paid on PC/console [SECONDARY].

### Platform convergences — what these platforms teach that mobile/physical didn't
1. **Player-generated content is the moment engine AND the content-supply answer** (Jackbox; Among Us). Gate #4's strongest observed solution: the group authors infinite unrepeatable content for free.
2. **The audience mechanic solves passive participation** (Jackbox: spectators vote and swing scoring). A third participation tier between player and absentee — directly relevant to the #3/#6 collision.
3. **Zero-install onboarding wins** (Jackbox: one owner + room code + phones; ~a year of engineering to avoid app downloads). Onboarding friction is a design variable, not a given.
4. **Designed-for-two is a mass market** (Hazelight 50M cumulative). Strongest support for the separate-couples-edition resolution.
5. **Friend's Pass = one purchase covers the pair at AAA scale** — finding #7's hygiene constraint with the biggest proof available.
6. **Asymmetric information roles force the retelling** (Keep Talking, We Were Here) — findings #3 and #5 literalized as mechanics.
7. **The archive should be a place, not a list** (Stardew farm, Minecraft builds) — finding #8 evolved.
8. **Nobody on these platforms does capture/share well** — Jackbox has no keepsake; Among Us memories live in memes. Manufacture + capture + share remains unoccupied here too.
