## Per product

### Paired (public surfaces; in-app internals NOT experienced)
- **Core loop as presented:** both partners answer the same daily question; answers stay hidden until both answer, then reveal side-by-side. Answer-lock confirmed in Play listing copy [REAL: listing read 2026-10-03].
- **Tone:** premium pitch leans therapeutic-expert ("therapist-written"); App Store screenshots sell calm intimacy, not play.
- **Designed memorable moment:** the side-by-side reveal of hidden answers — anticipation of the partner's answer is the product's emotional peak.
- **Friction points (from surfaces):** premium paywall gates the loop; the ritual's in-app feel, pacing, and notification pressure could not be evaluated without an account — marked second-hand.
- **Honesty note:** all claims about the answer-lock *experience* (not its existence) remain second-hand.

### OurCouple — Date Night Generator (actually tried)
- **Core loop as experienced:** one tap → instant date card. Unlimited free spins.
- **Tone:** frictionless, casual, zero-friction fun.
- **Designed memorable moment:** none, really — the card arrives with no stake.
- **Friction points:** the absence of friction IS the finding. **Zero commitment: unlimited spins, no lock-in, no reveal, no record.** Tried first-hand: it is the cautionary shape — reveal without stake is a button tap, and a mission you can re-spin forever is not a commitment device. Directly informs synthesis finding #1's gate.

### The Adventure Challenge (site walk; physical product NOT handled)
- **Core loop as presented:** "Your scratch-off guide to adventure" — scratch a mission, you must do it ("no backing out"), journal/photo pages capture it in the same book. Editions: Couples, Family, Friends, Solo, En Español, Couple vs Couple. Couples Edition $49.99 [REAL: store read 2026-10-03].
- **Tone:** gift-framed adventure; the site sells the *idea* of 50 surprises more than any single mission.
- **Designed memorable moment:** the scratch itself — tactile irreversibility converting a suggestion into a commitment.
- **Friction points:** the scratch ritual's actual feel (effort, sound, irreversibility, the journaling moment) cannot be evaluated through a screen — the single most load-bearing unknown in the package, marked not-first-hand-tested. Staged ask: order the physical deck.

### We're Not Really Strangers (site walk; not played)
- **Core loop as presented:** 150 cards across three escalating levels (Perception → Connection → Reflection) + wildcards + Final Card.
- **Tone:** vulnerable, aesthetic, confessional — the brand is the vibe as much as the cards.
- **Designed memorable moment:** the Final Card — a designed ending beat (consistent with synthesis finding #2: design the ending).
- **Friction points:** conversation-depth product, not a moment-creation product — talk about existing connection vs. manufacturing new shared experiences (different job; track1 F4). Not played first-hand; play feel remains second-hand.

## The blunt gap (from the walkthrough)

**No digital product walked has built the combination of: (1) blind-mutual-reveal** (both commit before either sees), **+ (2) a shared trophy case** (captures accumulate into a co-owned visible archive), **+ (3) a real stake mechanic** (limited rounds, a timer, or a forfeit that makes each round unrepeatable). Each element exists somewhere — answer-lock (Paired), archives (Between, FamilyAlbum), commitment (Adventure Challenge, physical only) — but the three together, digitally, are unbuilt. That combination is the design opening. It is also almost entirely [OPEN]: no walked product proves the digital transfer of any of the three.
