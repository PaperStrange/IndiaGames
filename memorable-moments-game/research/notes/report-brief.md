# Report brief — illustrated research report (for the page builder)

Read every markdown file in this research directory first: `track1-products.md`,
`track1-addendum.md` (Sections A–F; Section F holds the first-hand browser numbers),
`track2-psychology.md`, `track3-market.md`, `track4-tech.md`, `synthesis.md` (revised),
and `notes/self-questioning.md`. The synthesis is the decision backbone — the page
must present DECISIONS, not prose.

## What this page is
A polished, visual, decision-first RESEARCH REPORT on a digital-first game concept
that helps friends, family, and date-mates CREATE memorable moments together, then
capture and share them. It is NOT a game design, NOT a PRD, NOT a pitch deck.
No unvalidated proposal may be presented as a solution. Every claim carries an
evidence label: [REAL] (verified live in browser 2026-10-03), [SECONDARY] (published
source), [OPEN] (unvalidated).

## Audience and tone
Sharp, decision-first, visual. Diagrams and tables over paragraphs. The reader is a
product manager who wants: what did we learn, what does it decide, what is still open.

## Required sections, in this order
1. **Cover** — title, date 2026-10-03, one-line verdict: digital-first; the
   manufacture → capture → share loop is unoccupied across 33 mapped products (wider screened universe is larger — see appendix).
2. **Executive decision map** — the 8 findings from synthesis.md as cards; each card:
   finding → evidence (labeled) → the DECISION it drives. No finding without a decision.
3. **Competitor landscape** — embed `assets/illustrations/landscape.svg` (33 products
   mapped: manufacturing vs keeping × physical vs digital; the empty top-right quadrant
   is the whitespace). Below it, a compact table of the headline competitors with
   traction figures: Adventure Challenge (5M+ products sold claim [SECONDARY]),
   Paired (1M+ Play downloads / 8M cross-platform claim), Between (35M+ couples claim),
   FamilyAlbum (10M+ Play downloads), It Takes Two (Overwhelmingly Positive, 48,219
   English reviews [REAL]), Jackbox (1B+ user sessions claim [SECONDARY]).
4. **Platform expansion** — what Steam / itch.io / Jackbox taught that mobile didn't:
   player-generated content as the content engine AND content-supply answer (Jackbox);
   designed-for-two is mass market (It Takes Two 30M [SECONDARY]); Friend's Pass =
   one purchase covers the pair; audience/QR-code join for passive participants;
   compatibility-as-scoreboard as digital blind-reveal precedent (Sync or Swim);
   Steam has no "couples" tag — date-night games are undiscoverable there.
5. **First-hand testing boundary** — an honest box: what was personally walked in the
   live browser (7 product sites + OurCouple date generator tried directly + Steam/
   itch.io/Jackbox sweeps) vs what was NOT tested (no accounts, no installs, no
   partner play, no purchases). Name the boundary plainly.
6. **Snapshot gallery** — embed all JPGs in `assets/snapshots/web/` (10 images),
   each with a caption: product name, source URL, captured 2026-10-03.
7. **Mechanism diagrams** — embed the five SVGs in `assets/illustrations/`:
   `reveal-loop.svg`, `peak-end.svg`, `collision.svg`, `gates.svg`, `digital-gap.svg`,
   each with a one-paragraph decision caption.
8. **The open collision** — synchrony needs active players; passive-partner attrition
   is the best-documented failure mode. First pilot must test one passive player vs
   two active players. Plus the ranked open gates from synthesis.md.
9. **Evidence & sources appendix** — every figure with its label and source;
   list what is [OPEN].

## Design
Clean, modern, generous whitespace, mobile-responsive. Sticky section nav.
Color accents: indigo (#4f46e5) for digital, rose (#e11d48) for physical,
teal (#0d9488) for keeping/archive. Evidence labels as small pills.
All images local (embedded from the assets folders) — the page must work fully offline.
