01 / 08

Executive decision map

Eight findings. Eight moves.

Each conclusion terminates in a product decision. Evidence labels describe verification effort, not scientific strength.

01 · COMMITMENT

Locked reveal leads; digital mission stakes remain unproven.

The Adventure Challenge’s 5M+ claim and Paired’s 1M+ Play floor / 8M cross-platform claim were read in-run REAL; transfer to digital mission stakes remains untested OPEN. OurCouple’s unlimited re-spin had no stake.

DECISIONLead with mission reveal. Gate whether a timer, limited round, or mutual lock recreates scratch-off commitment digitally.
02 · MEMORY ARC

Design the peak and the ending; do not pad.

Peak–end and duration-neglect studies govern remembered utility of bounded episodes; transfer from pain studies to positive social play remains open. REAL OPEN transfer

DECISIONShort sessions, one intensity peak, one warm shared ending. Long products may still be composed of short sessions.
03 · BONDING

Synchronous joint action beats parallel activity.

Wiltermuth & Heath found that moving in sync increased cooperation and closeness without increasing happiness REAL. Pizarro’s meta-analysis supports perceived shared emotion as a mechanism SECONDARY.

DECISIONUse common time windows, countdowns and simultaneous reveals. Favor mild shared effort over frictionless pleasantness.
04 · CAPTURE

Capture deliberately, and sparsely per moment.

Mindless photo-taking impaired recall; focused, intentional capture removed the effect. No paper tested “one shot” as the optimum. REAL OPEN optimum

DECISIONPrompt a focused capture beat. Never auto-record. Keep spoken content outside heavy photo moments.
05 · RETELLING

The retelling is part of the encoding.

Conversation reshapes memory; auto-recaps have never been tested against co-retelling. FamilyAlbum shows auto artifacts can still work as keepsakes. SECONDARY

DECISIONRequire a shared retelling beat. Let automated recaps prompt that exchange, not replace it.
06 · MOTIVATION

One reluctant partner is the best-documented failure mode.

Paired review patterns, streak guilt and Official’s shutdown all expose participation asymmetry. “Tap, don’t author” is an untested requirement. SECONDARY OPEN response

DECISIONPilot passive vs active pairs before settling the loop. If passive play does not bond, narrow the market to already-motivated groups.
07 · ECONOMICS

Free core + one subscription per pair is the sampled digital norm.

Live digital players in the sample cluster at roughly $5–12/month; no one-time digital model was observed in this sample. Content paywalls draw complaints. SECONDARY

DECISIONKeep play free. Test premium features and keepsakes; never double-charge a pair. Physical pricing remains a separate one-time model.
08 · ARCHIVE

Export is day-one trust; archive is a year-two asset.

Between’s listing claims 35M couples; Cluster’s event pooling died after days; Official stranded memories. BackThen exhibits the subscription + printed-keepsake pattern SECONDARY; attach rate remains [OPEN].

DECISIONShip export immediately. Treat the archive as a place that grows, not a launch-retention answer or a scrolling list.
Central constraint

Synchrony asks everyone to act; attrition asks the loop to survive someone passive. Both cannot be requirements without a test. This collision is the first pilot’s core experiment.

03 / 08

Beyond Steam, beyond mobile

Every platform changes the answer.

The expanded sweep covers four platform families: Mobile / Physical / PC & console / Web & itch.io.

30M

It Takes Two copies, studio announcement SECONDARY. A relationship-centered two-player game is not automatically niche.

∞

Jackbox’s real content library is the room. Prompts frame the action; players’ answers, drawings and votes make every round unique.

Jackbox · web / TV

One host owns the pack; guests join on phones via a 4-letter room code. Secret input → shared reveal → peer vote. Audience members can participate without performing.

Use player-generated content as the supply engine; test audience voting as the passive-partner tier.

Hazelight · console / PC

It Takes Two, A Way Out and Split Fiction make two-person dependence the core. Friend’s Pass lets the second player join free.

Separate the couples edition, and make one purchase cover the pair. Constantly changing verbs are the anti-exhaustion model.

Keep Talking · PC / VR

Asymmetric information forces precise communication under a timer. The manual-holder is not passive: the role is lighter, but indispensable.

Test asymmetric roles before accepting tap-only participation. Shared effort can vary without disappearing.

itch.io · indie / browser

INREDA makes couples negotiate over one controller; Sync or Swim turns compatibility into the score; Couple Storm withholds partner information; Count Together rewards synchronization.

Prototype with hard constraints. Compatibility-as-scoreboard is a useful blind-reveal precedent, but the scene has no traction proof.

Among Us · cross-platform

The emergency meeting is an inbuilt retelling ritual: players reconstruct an event, argue, vote, and create the story themselves.

Emergent social rules can replace authored content — but capture remains missing.

Stardew / Minecraft

A shared farm or build is a persistent co-owned artifact. Players inhabit their accumulation rather than browse it.

Treat the archive as a place that visibly grows, not a chronological feed.

First-hand test boundary

What was walked — and what was not.

Public pages were inspected in the live browser on 2026-10-03. This is not equivalent to installing, buying or playing with another person.

Personally walked

  • Paired public site, premium pitch and App Store screenshots
  • The Adventure Challenge editions, pricing and scratch presentation
  • We’re Not Really Strangers levels, tone and pricing
  • OurCouple Date Night Generator — one-tap flow tried directly
  • Steam pages for 10 co-op/party titles
  • itch.io couples and party-game listings
  • Jackbox site, price ladder, join flow and audience mechanic

Not tested

  • No paid purchases or physical product handling
  • No partner play, group play or observed room dynamics
  • No Paired account; answer-lock feel remains unseen
  • No app installs or authenticated in-app flows
  • No YouTube playthroughs; unavailable from the research network
  • No tactile scratch validation
  • No digital mission product with proven commercial traction was found
DECISIONNo digital-commitment conclusion marked [OPEN] above may be cited without this boundary.

07 / 08

Threat-ranked gates

Do not call it solved until these tests pass.

The sequence protects against polishing downstream mechanics around an unproven core.

First experiment: passive vs active.

Run the same bounded mission with two active players and with one player limited to a lighter role. Compare closeness, session completion and 48-hour recall. If lighter participation finishes the session but fails to form a shared memory, it is not a solution.
