# Game images for the studio hub

These files are copied byte-for-byte from the repository. They are presentation
assets, not new gameplay verification or human playtest evidence. No image was
generated, retouched or cropped for this dashboard.

| Asset | What it shows | Evidence date |
|---|---|---|
| `small-hours-opening.png` | Automated capture of the Small Hours opening screen | 5 October 2026 |
| `vivid-iona-opening.png` | Automated capture of Iona's opening scene in Vivid 0.1.2 | 27 October 2026 |
| `bell-town-map.webp` | Original town-map artwork used in The Thirteenth Bell | Artwork; no capture date claimed |

The complete immutable source paths, source commits, dimensions, byte counts,
SHA-256 hashes, captions and alternative text are in
[the presentation manifest](../../data/hub-presentation.json). Each original
remains available through its pinned `source_url`. `captured_at` is null because
the records date the evidence runs, not an individual frame's exact capture time.

The images show opening material and the town map without exposing the selected
Mina-route solution or story endings. Screenshots are agent-operated captures;
they do not depict human participants. Preserve each game's existing content and
art rights when reusing these files; this hub does not grant a new asset license.
