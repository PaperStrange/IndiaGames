## Session

1. Obtain willing participation and explain stopping. Avoid collecting sensitive personal details or artifacts by default.
2. Record device, familiarity, prior exposure, condition/order and build. A pair means two actual people.
3. Give the agreed entry instruction. Observe before offering help; record every intervention.
4. At selected natural moments, capture a prediction before action without naming the intended solution.
5. Record completion, confusion/recovery, actual stopping and optional keep/replay choice. Keep spontaneous and prompted actions separate.
6. Ask neutral questions: “What changed?”, “What do you think caused that?”, “What would you do next?” Record consented words separately from interpretation.
