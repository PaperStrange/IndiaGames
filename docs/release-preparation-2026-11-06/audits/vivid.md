## Critical journeys for the proposed language/device matrix

Each row needs a recorded result for every supported locale on the chosen device/browser combinations; blank means untested. Use language QA and functional/device QA as separate evidence dimensions. These checks do not replace fresh-player cohorts.

| Journey | What can fail | Required observation |
|---|---|---|
| Gallery → either story, language selection, bookmark/reload | Mixed languages, missing glyphs, inaccessible picker, lost story/profile | Exact locale/resource revision and announced document language; complete labels; stable route and focus |
| Iona clues → correct/wrong signal → recollection | Translation accidentally gives/removes clue; short/long or east/west meaning changes | Player prediction, action, visible result, recovery and original-language quotation; no added coaching |
| Iona private/public dedication → return → placement | Consent difference lost; memory disappears on origin or device change; touch drag blocks scroll | Preserve same browser/profile/origin for the scheduled return; record tap/keyboard equivalents and actual placement; do not imply cross-device/cloud save |
| Mara three inspections → alignment at notch 3 → any interpretation | CJK text hides controls; touch/keyboard slider differs; one translation implies a preferred emotion | All controls reachable; registration consequence understood; any interpretation accepted without moral/scoring hint |
| Phone/tablet rotation, split view, background/resume, second finger or interrupted drag | Input cancellation, stale pointer capture, focus/state loss, layout clipping | Real iPhone/iPad Safari and Android phone/tablet Chrome where selected; record exact hardware and software, not just viewport |
| 3D/reduced motion/flat fallback, cold load and active manipulation | WebGL/context failure, resource pressure, thermal stalls, false FPS claims | Complete both stories in flat mode; retain physical 3D measurements separately. Existing method calls for three 30-second active samples, provisional ≥30 FPS,≤8 MiB selected-experience cold bytes,≤100k triangles and DPR ≤1.5; document measurement scope and leave unavailable numbers unverified |
| Recovery/reset with assistive technology | Wrong-language announcements, wrong-story erase, lost original save | VoiceOver/TalkBack or relevant desktop screen reader with fluent operator; confirm reset/recovery scope and actual keyboard hardware separately |

## Existing human gate remains intact

The [current handoff:63](https://github.com/PaperStrange/IndiaGames/blob/2130e5523dbf9ba27af268f0ba8cccb86aef56e2/docs/gamejam/maintenance-2026-11-06/HUMAN-HANDOFF.md#L63) requires six fresh Iona participants and six different Mara participants on the exact build. Iona: 15-minute unaided window including same-session return/placement and at least 4/6 completing plus explaining choice→return; at most 1/6 expects unrestricted AI. Mara: 10-minute window and at least 4/6 completing plus explaining action→visible consequence. Keep all assisted/failed/abandoned attempts. Both need zero unresolved critical bugs and an independent human reviewer who plays the build and is neither participant nor observer. Optional neutral and 48–72-hour recall studies remain separate.

Translation and device checks may share logistics, but a fluent translator or repeated QA operator is not automatically a fresh study participant. Record language proficiency and any language help. Localized prompts must preserve the original neutral instructions and timing; do not silently change thresholds to accommodate longer copy. Where extra language-specific cohorts or changed timing are proposed, settle and preregister that protocol explicitly.

The existing [physical-device method:5](https://github.com/PaperStrange/IndiaGames/blob/2130e5523dbf9ba27af268f0ba8cccb86aef56e2/docs/gamejam/vivid-iteration-2026-10-21/human-study/devices-and-recall.md#L5) already separates real hardware from emulation. [Same-origin return and private serving:57](https://github.com/PaperStrange/IndiaGames/blob/2130e5523dbf9ba27af268f0ba8cccb86aef56e2/docs/gamejam/maintenance-2026-11-06/HUMAN-HANDOFF.md#L57) matter on phones: phone loopback is not the facilitator machine. Agree a reachable static-build-only test URL and keep participant records outside the served folder. Desktop-to-phone resume is not implemented by the device-local save contract.
