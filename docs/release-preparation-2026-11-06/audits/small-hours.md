## What existing evidence does and does not cover

The filed SH-23 record reports 13 unit tests, two identical development exports, 18 HTTP file comparisons, 66 Chromium browser checks across root/nested mounts, and three timer groups/nine assertions. It includes 390px touch emulation and a synthetic iframe, a real 45,051ms expiry, clock-assisted cancellation and synthetic lifecycle events. Four real favicon 404 responses were retained, with no other observed errors. This audit cites that historical result; it does not rerun it. [S9]

The runner imports Chromium specifically. That report supplies no Japanese/Chinese validation, Firefox/WebKit result, physical phone/tablet test, screen-reader observation, actual hosted portal test or willing-pair experience. The synthetic tablet layout evidence for the main dashboard must not be relabeled as Small Hours game-device evidence. [S10]

These proposals add engineering preparation; they do not unconditionally activate SH-11's human-informed gameplay experiment or alter its dependencies. SH-11/12 and release staging/promotion remain conditional. Source inspection, catalog specification and test/template preparation can proceed without recruitment after plan settlement. Language quality and claimed physical support cannot be signed off by agents alone.

## Critical player journeys for the eventual matrix

1. Choose `en`, `ja` or `zh-Hans`; understand that two willing people share one screen and may leave. Verify the language of visible and accessible labels.
2. Person A chooses/guesses, seals, hands over; B cannot see A's private values. Both understand the curtain is courtesy, not device security.
3. Reveal together, perform each person's offscreen contribution, and leave the optional timer unstarted or allow it to expire without ending play.
4. Each partner composes Japanese/Chinese text with a real IME; cancel a candidate, commit it, type/paste at the limit, or leave blank. The virtual keyboard must not block the action or expose the other person's private step.
5. Agree before saving; confirm the actual SVG arrives, renders CJK/emoji/date labels, and opens/prints without clipped text. Distinguish offered, attempted, delivered and voluntarily chosen save.
6. Cancel End & forget, then confirm it; change scene/replay, rotate/split view, background/resume and navigate browser history. Record actual privacy clearing/reset behavior; do not expect saved SVG files to be deleted.

For every claimed locale/device combination, all blocking entry, privacy, exit, readable-control and download issues need an explicit disposition. Pairwise selection may reduce noncritical layout permutations; it cannot excuse an untested critical path or turn emulation into physical evidence.

## Preserve the existing human gate

The canonical baseline requires six willing pairs/12 distinct people, preregistration before play, actual device setup and all first-six attempts retained. Thresholds stay **5/6 unaided reveals, 4/6 pairs with both contributing to the offscreen activity, 3/6 voluntary save/replay**. The independent reviewer cannot be a participant or the observer whose evidence they approve. Contacts, consent mapping, recordings and personal keepsakes remain private. No booking or result exists merely because a template was prepared. [S11]
