# Alpha 0.2 material assets

Generated for this project with the built-in image-generation tool on 2026-09-08, one generation per asset. WebP files are delivery encodings of the generated PNGs; no content edits.

**vellum-alpha-02.webp** — Portrait 2:3 flat material scan of blank antique sheepskin vellum, warm pale honey ivory, natural subtle fibers and pores, delicate undulations, restrained spotting, even diffuse light. No writing, symbols, illustrations, borders, folds, tears, perspective, or objects. Texture fills the frame.

**leather-alpha-02.webp** — Square macro material scan of dark espresso aged bookbinding leather, fine natural pores and subtly worn highlights, flat framing and even low light. No seams, embossing, decoration, text, border, or objects.

Live light, page edge geometry, the gutter, and the bookmark are separate CSS layers. The image assets contain no text or interface controls. Both are served locally and copied into the mobile bundle.
