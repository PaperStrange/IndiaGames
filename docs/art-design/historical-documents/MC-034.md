# MC-034 — Evaluate one tangible 3D writing spread

Type: Technical investigation. Priority: P1. Status: Proposed. Planning wave: Sprint 02 selected forecast; first removed if capacity is insufficient. Owner roles: Rendering + Design; people unassigned. Time budget: 2 engineer-days including device measurements and report; design input shares MC-035's allocation. No additional QA days are assumed. Readiness: geometry/input interface review, MC-035 dimension sheet, available physical devices. Related: MC-006, MC-021, MC-028, MC-032, MC-033.

As a writer, I want the digital book to share the physical notebook's proportions and behave convincingly as I turn and write, so that the two editions feel connected.

## Investigation completion criteria

- [ ] AC1. Deliver one inspectable prototype spread with cover/spine thickness, red ribbon, curved sheet deformation, page contact shadow and stable writing view. Record units and MC-035 dimensions. Verify provenance and reuse terms for imported code/assets.
- [ ] AC2. Demonstrate corner drag, release/cancel, forward/backward turn, text placement and regional erasure. Ink mapping stays correct on deformed or settled surfaces. GPU deformation is not assumed to update pointer hit-testing. Compare with the existing editor using identical inputs.
- [ ] AC3. Measure frame pacing, pointer-to-ink timing, asset size, memory where accessible and ten-minute behavior on a named desktop, physical iPad and Android phone. Proposed targets: at least 30 frames/second during turns, p95 pointer-to-ink at most 50 ms, zero lost/shifted input in recorded journeys. Disclose unavailable measurements; these are evaluation targets, not current claims.
- [ ] AC4. Reduced motion, keyboard focus, accessible text, 200% text enlargement, hidden tabs and unavailable graphics retain usable writing. Effects do not intercept capture controls or writing gestures.
- [ ] AC5. Deliver an evidenced adopt/rework decision, exact source/device/browser versions, recording and unresolved issues. An investigation can complete with a measured negative result. It does not close MC-028 or authorize production renderer replacement.

## Deliverables

Prototype source/model assets, measurement report, input mapping explanation and adoption decision. Evaluate Three.js/React Three Fiber with document/ink state independent of geometry. Prototype the visible spread and turning sheet through a reviewed page-content interface. Direct editing accuracy is part of the investigation. Missing required devices or design input leaves those criteria blocked; a desktop image alone is insufficient.
