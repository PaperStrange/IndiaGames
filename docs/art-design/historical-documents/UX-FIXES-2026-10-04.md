## Preserved interaction rules

- Erasing or rewriting does not cancel running recognition jobs. Revision checks prevent stale results from reviving changed ink.
- Recognition and fading start at the same input-end event. Replies require both completed recognition and the full fade interval.
- Erasing during fading restores visibility and then removes the touched region.
- Original ink remains recoverable. Restoring an exchange removes its reply fragments across all continuation leaves.
- Provider mode never silently switches to device OCR after a provider/configuration error; ink remains available for correction or retry.
- The fixed paper can still scroll within the viewport. A leaf does not grow to fit a reply.

## Remaining evidence and limits

- Live provider transcription quality, latency, token cost, fallback failures and long streaming replies require a configured provider. The browser sample validates rendering and persistence, not AI quality.
- The browser had no WebGL context. Its CSS fallback was visually inspected; the revised shader path still requires browser/device visual QA.
- Physical iOS/Android input, IME, touch correction, reduced motion, battery/frame behavior and app-store packaging remain open. A successful mobile bundle is not physical-device verification.
- Table pagination preserves rows; unusually wide cells still need a separate column-wrapping design.
- Full M0 recovery, multi-device sync/conflicts, source-linked recall and safety/privacy gates remain open.
- This lighting is a portable real-time shading illusion; it is not ray tracing or a claim of film-equivalent fidelity.
