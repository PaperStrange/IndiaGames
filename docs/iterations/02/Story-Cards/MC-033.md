# MC-033 — Receive a reply tied to my captured handwriting

Type: Story. Priority: P0 for the paper journey. Status: In progress. Planning wave: Sprint 02 selected forecast. Owner roles: AI + Client + QA; people unassigned. Allocation: 3 engineer-days and 1 QA day. Dependencies: MC-032 and B3 live-provider/source/measurement boundary. Live-provider acceptance remains blocked. Related: MC-005, MC-006, MC-008, MC-009, MC-011, MC-012, MC-017, MC-018, MC-021, MC-022.

As a writer, I want the companion to interpret the page I selected, answer it, and let me revisit the original, so that the conversation remains connected to what I actually wrote.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] AC1. Explicit Ask creates one revision-bound turn after durable capture. Vision recognition and five-second digital fade share input-end time. Preview, camera pauses and navigation cannot submit. The original stays accessible; physical ink is unchanged.
- [ ] AC2. Provider mode uses a configured vision route with model lock and budget enforced. Non-provider mode can use local OCR and identifies that mode. Missing capability, revoked connection, timeout and failed recognition preserve the original and explain the failure; no invented response appears.
- [ ] AC3. Interpretation preserves names, numbers, negation and uncertainty. Editing requires no compulsory confirmation dialog. Correction/retake preserves historical sources; running jobs finish without applying to superseded revisions. Correction requires explicit resubmission for a new turn.
- [ ] AC4. Replies address the current page; recalled claims resolve to permitted source/revision. Unsupported claims are identified. Current recognition, fade and pre-reveal content checks gate display. Source actions never expose another owner's page.
- [ ] AC5. Long replies use fixed continuation leaves without changing dimensions or covering writing. Provider/context/page limits retain the complete original and disclose the limit. Background results cannot overwrite another page or draft.
- [ ] AC6. At least 30 real photo-to-reply turns cover declared devices, handwriting styles, lighting/orientation, names, negation, numerals, correction and retake. Record reference transcription, errors, usable replies, all failed runs and provider usage. This set does not close MC-022's broader benchmark.
- [ ] AC7. Report p50/p95 Ask-to-first-visible-reply including fade for the ordinary short-turn subset; retain MC-012's p95 target of at most 12 seconds. Include count, cold/warm state, recognition/retry/content-check charges and billing gaps. Capture-to-Ask is a separate effort measure. A missed target stays open.

## Implementation and evidence

Pass source ID, image revision and turn ID through recognition, provider execution, memory and display. Keep original, interpretation and response as separate records. Reuse provider adapters, scoped context, budgeting and ink revision behavior. A photo cannot establish historical pen order.

Evidence: actual input/output comparison, source navigation, corrected-source recall, out-of-order jobs, failed/denied providers, fixed-leaf recording and measured usage. Actual provider/spend, reviewed processing and device access are readiness requirements. MC-008/011/012/018/021/022 retain their full criteria.

Candidate implementation connects explicit Ask to server-loaded vision input, five-second fade, current-page/result guards, optional transcription correction, exact original source links and existing fixed-leaf replies. TypeScript/build and existing fixed-page/ink regressions pass. The storage integration rejects a mismatched notebook and reports a missing provider truthfully. No actual photo-to-provider reply, recognition accuracy, pre-reveal screening, latency/cost distribution or physical-device result is claimed. Photo-region erasure and independent original/transcription/reply revision history need further work. Full criteria remain unchecked; see [candidate evidence](../RECOVERY-CHECKPOINT.md).
