# MC-035 — Specify the paper notebook and its matching digital edition

Type: Design/enabler. Priority: P1. Status: Proposed. Planning wave: Sprint 02 selected forecast, dependent on design capacity. Owner roles: Book/product design + Rendering; people unassigned. Allocation: 2 specialist days shared with MC-034. Related: MC-028, MC-032, MC-034. Dimension sheet dependency: none; provide it before MC-034 integration.

As a notebook owner, I want the physical notebook and the app's book to share a recognizable design, so that moving between them feels like using the same object.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] AC1. Provide an A5-sized paper design target in millimeters: cover/paper dimensions, binding method, opening angle, red ribbon, materials and page labels. Distinguish proposed dimensions from measurements.
- [ ] AC2. Specify sewn paper sections and a flexible binding joint. Identify optional printed inserts, ornament or embossing tools; writing pages remain paper. Cover thickness and binding clearance require actual fit validation.
- [ ] AC3. Deliver shared parameter JSON and annotated views. For printable components, supply dimensioned STL/3MF, units, slicer inspection and geometry checks. For conventional binding parts, supply cutting/assembly drawings. Digital rendering does not establish physical fit.
- [ ] AC4. Provide a bill of materials, assembly steps, supplier/printer requirements and a physical evaluation procedure covering opening/closing, twenty-minute writing comfort, ribbon placement and repeated turns. Record actual results only when a sample exists; otherwise fabrication/fit validation remains pending.
- [ ] AC5. Notebook/page pairing identifies an object while authenticated access stays separate. Use original MentalCity artwork with documented provenance. Explain that animated ink and companion replies appear in the app.

## Completion boundary

This card delivers a reviewable design specification. Manufactured fit, durability, production cost, demand and a physical prototype need separate evidence. A physical sample and fit review precede manufacturing or a customer study using this edition. Quotes identify supplier, quantity, date, shipping/tax assumptions and unknowns.

Deliverables: dimension sheet, parameter JSON, annotated views, assembly drawings, bill of materials and physical evaluation checklist. These are future sprint deliverables, not assets created by the planning update.
