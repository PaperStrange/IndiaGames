## What the Notebook edition changes

One authored chapter uses three exchanges and two endings. Marking a phrase changes the reply; the player can recover their original wording, compare accounts and decide what survives. Ten–fifteen minutes is a target awaiting human observation. The static game does not expose private notebook services or pretend its responses are generated AI. Story/cast/action IDs and language catalogs stay separate from the engine.

The [six actual public-game observations](https://github.com/PaperStrange/IndiaGames/blob/9330f88fd9960ce30f9de1490ccf4870e10cac1e/docs/gamejam/competitor-plays-2026-11-14/README.md) cover narrative fiction, resource decisions, simulation, quick consequences and attention. They are agent-operated research, separate from the previously supplied owner-confirmed human records and historical roster counts.

| Observed game | Actual stopping point and limit | Notebook implication to test |
|---|---|---|
| A Dark Room | Fire, stranger, forest and gathering upgrades; opening only | Let a small action reveal a new relationship |
| Seedship | One completed three-planet mission and saved outcome; not all endings | Make earlier sacrifices legible in the final account |

Each record separates actual actions, published criticism and player/design/art/accessibility/operator interpretation. These observations support design hypotheses; they do not prove fun, human demand or six complete playthroughs. Tool/interleaved elapsed time is not human session duration.
