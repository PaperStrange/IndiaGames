The seven planned browser games are Line Rider, Sandspiel, Townscaper, shapez, Universal Paperclips, A Dance of Fire and Ice and Draw a Stickman. The current round attempts four of these: Sandspiel, Townscaper, shapez and A Dance of Fire and Ice. Its additional Celeste Classic and Sort the Court observations do not automatically complete the original seven-title contract; Sort the Court is a repeat from November 14. The filed records distinguish actual actions, incomplete endpoints and assistance. The November 14 six-game studies remain separate agent-operated observations with their original bounded endpoints.

The owner's latest feedback asks for clearer how/why-to-play, less repetitive story loops and more distinctive interaction, with accessible online previews. These are reported design problems to investigate through play, reviews and discussions before selecting a mechanism. They are not proof that a particular proposed ink system will solve the problem.

The sequence is **source review → market observations → three contrasting experiments → evidence-based core decision → implementation and qualification**. Material play, construction, ecology, graphic programming, performance, restoration, communication and economy are alternatives to compare; they are not an eight-system implementation commitment. Narrative and cast remain flexible, and a page may persist and evolve across play.
