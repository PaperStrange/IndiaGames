# Notebook ink redesign · 15 November 2026

The next game should retain the original notebook’s direct writing, erasure, fading and recoverable marks, then make those marks produce understandable gameplay consequences. The owner’s correction supersedes phrase selection and one-page-per-scene as requirements for the future design.

The seven planned browser games are Line Rider, Sandspiel, Townscaper, shapez, Universal Paperclips, A Dance of Fire and Ice and Draw a Stickman. No new session is counted as played. The November 14 six-game studies remain separate agent-operated observations with their original bounded endpoints.

The sequence is **source review → market observations → three contrasting experiments → evidence-based core decision → implementation and qualification**. Material play, construction, ecology, graphic programming, performance, restoration, communication and economy are alternatives to compare; they are not an eight-system implementation commitment. Narrative and cast remain flexible, and a page may persist and evolve across play.

## Baseline and release boundary

[Play the preserved 0.1.0 baseline](https://paperstrange.github.io/IndiaGames/development/toms-notebook/0.1.0/). It is the authored-choice comparison build, not the redesigned game. Its source, ten-file inventory and November 14 engineering checks retain their exact identities. Original notebook product 0.4.0-alpha.0 remains separate.

November 16 is the individual go/defer checkpoint. November 20 requires English, Japanese and Simplified Chinese, declared physical desktop/phone/tablet coverage and independent exact-build human approval. Incomplete games defer. The baseline’s draft authored-choice protocol cannot automatically qualify a different core. PR17 adoption and effective branch protection remain separate open gates.
