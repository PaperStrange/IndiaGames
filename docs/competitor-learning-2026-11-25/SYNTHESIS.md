# What the observations change

These are design hypotheses derived from bounded agent-operated attempts and a small, deliberately contrasting sample of public opinions. They do not establish what all players enjoy, commercial demand, or market uniqueness. The owner's three complaints are feedback to investigate; the underlying group-chat messages were not independently obtained.

## Criticize the evidence before copying the game

| Lens | Finding to keep | Shortcut to reject |
|---|---|---|
| Player | A legible action and an appealing reason to act are separate problems. A known research task can hide an empty opening. | The operator completed a route, therefore a new player will understand or want it. |
| Systems | A stable input can support changing decisions: [Sort's yes/no choices](records/sort-court.md) affect different people and resources; [shapez's placement](records/shapez.md) leads to a new flow problem. | Every story needs a new gesture, or more buttons automatically make the loop interesting. |
| Narrative | Visible consequences can carry the story. A person benefiting from an intervention can give the next action a stake. | Replace character text or add lore while the player's decision remains identical. |
| Art / interaction | [Sandspiel's material states](records/sandspiel.md) and [Townscaper's generated architectural changes](records/townscaper.md) make actions visible. | Pretty particles or procedural decoration alone demonstrate meaningful authorship. Precision, discoverability and recovery still matter. |
| Operator | A public entry, working interaction, readable failure, saved state and a return visit are different stages. | A deployed URL or passing browser suite establishes a complete playable journey or a physical-device release. |
| Research | Positive and critical player accounts can disagree because they want different kinds of play. | Average unlike ratings into a fun score, mistake selected comments for representative demand, or transfer full-edition reviews to a small web demo. |

[Celeste Classic](records/celeste.md) supplies a concrete example of revising the use of an existing action, with pause assistance and failures disclosed. Its control-discovery discussion also shows why high ratings do not clear onboarding. [ADOFAI](records/adofai.md) remained an entry/menu attempt: no tutorial, track, retry or audible assessment. Its reviews cannot fill that firsthand gap.

The new Small Hours and Bell bundles are useful execution records. They add **no new competitor sessions**. A one-operator enactment cannot validate two-person intimacy; three different scenes in three languages cannot isolate translation quality. Bell's completed English Contrast route does not close Original-mode or Japanese/Chinese journey coverage. Claims that the games only need recruitment, or that no competitor offers a similar experience, exceed that evidence. See [the audit](PROGRESS-AUDIT.md) for exact sources and document defects.

## Answers to the original three questions

The table separates a plausible player reason from a testable difference. “Distinctive experience” is a proposed contract to test, not a proven exclusive market position.

| Game | Why might someone want to play? | What could differentiate it on the browser platform? | Distinctive experience to test |
|---|---|---|---|
| Small Hours | Discover something about another person and make something neither would have made alone. | Let the second person's contribution transform the first within the play session; make joining and privacy boundaries easy to understand. A shareable artifact alone is insufficient differentiation. | A tiny shared memory whose playable situation and keepsake visibly depend on both people. Test with a real pair; a solo operator cannot stand in for this. |
| Vivid | Care about a person by changing a small, understandable situation in their world. | Distinct characters require different kinds of judgment, with consequences visible in the portrait scene. Avoid repeating inspect → correct setting → read payoff under new text. | A portrait becomes a responsive place: the player's intervention leaves a consequential trace, and a later return acknowledges what they actually changed. Test separately for each story; do not promise an unbuilt persistence system. |
| The Thirteenth Bell | Turn an initially puzzling event into a successful intervention through acquired knowledge. | Make knowledge change what the player can sensibly do; expose costs and consequences without repeating an entire known route. | The same town event is encountered again with a new causal understanding, allowing a different outcome. That is a specific hypothesis within an established time-loop genre, not an exclusive invention. |
| Tom's Notebook | Express, discover and develop skill through the player's own persistent marks. | The geometry, placement, connection, history or erasure of actual ink must affect play. Preserve writing and recovery rather than substitute authored phrase buttons. | A page remembers and responds to the player's marks, including revisions. Material, construction, ecology, programming, performance, restoration, communication and economy remain alternatives; no core is selected from this partial roster. |

The browser opportunity is a short path from an inviting premise to an actual action, with low setup and a clear way to return or share when relevant. The present project cannot claim this advantage across all four games: only Notebook has a current development preview on main. Publishing the other three is a delivery task; it will not repair a flat loop by itself.

## Revised sequence to consider after this research boundary

No row below is reported as implemented. The research changes the order of validation, not the human release requirements.

| Order / owner | Concrete next deliverable | Evidence that makes the next decision possible |
|---|---|---|
| 1 · Research + independent reviewer | Finish/reconcile Notebook's remaining Line Rider, Universal Paperclips (including separate next-day return) and Draw a Stickman comparisons. Resolve Sandspiel's unmatched comparison and ADOFAI's audio limits explicitly. | Attempted journeys and contrary findings; no silent replacement or seven-title completion claim. Populate the eight-family matrix before selecting three experiments. |
| 2 · Delivery / QA | Versioned development entry for each game, with current-build identity and matching instructions on the dashboard. Preserve the paused legacy candidate-URL repair as a separate request. | Test the real HTTPS entry, meaningful input and recovery on the hosted bytes; separate this from an official release. No new hosting was done in this research round. |
| 3 · Research / design | Observe a fresh, uncoached baseline for each game: entry → first action → consequence → confusion/recovery → offered stop. | Original actions, assistance and exact stopping point; record the player's reason for acting in their own words. Do not convert instructions followed into spontaneous motivation. |
| 4 · Vivid design / art / engineering | First bounded comparison: make one existing intervention visibly affect a person or the world, then offer a recoverable revision. Keep the story and cast model flexible. | Compare against the preserved baseline. Does the player explain what changed and form a new intention? If only visual spectacle improves, reconsider the interaction before expanding both stories. |
| 5 · Separate game agents | Small Hours: an early contribution-and-transformation pair loop. Bell: one early problem with clear arrival/commit/resolve/advance feedback, followed by a learned intervention. Notebook: three contrasting ink experiments selected only after the matrix review. | Separate game contracts and source branches; actual pair evidence for Small Hours. No automatic transfer of success between genres, stories or variants. |
| 6 · QA + humans + release owner | Validate the selected changes, stage immutable candidates on master, review all three languages and declared physical desktop/phone/tablet configurations, then decide each exact version. | English, Japanese and Simplified Chinese, actual human evidence and independent approval remain mandatory. Incomplete games defer. No replacement launch date is invented. |

Engineering and formative research can proceed without waiting for a thirty-person recruitment cohort. Small exploratory sessions can find design problems, but cannot replace the established release denominator or exact-build approval.

## Questions for the next human observation

This is a formative recording extension, **not** a new approved release threshold. Keep original notes, optional recordings with consent, locale, exact build, hardware/browser, assistance and participant role. Ask after the relevant action rather than leading the player before it.

| Concern | Observe first | Ask afterwards | Useful contrary evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| How / why | First purposeful action; hesitation; help used | “What were you trying to make happen? Why that?” | Can name the control but not the stake; acts only after instruction. |
| Flat loop | What information changes the second and third decision | “What changed your approach?” | Same response repeated despite a new character; skips payoff text; cannot explain the outcome. |
| Agency | Intended change, visible result and correction | “Which part of this result came from you?” | Credits decoration the system generated automatically; own marks do not affect behavior; cannot recover an unwanted result. |
| Continuation | Offer a real stop without reward or facilitator pressure | “Would you stop here or try something else? What?” | Wants closure, not another cycle; continuation is compliance with the task. |
| Pair play | Who initiates, changes or vetoes the shared result | Ask each person separately what they contributed | One person directs both; privacy or turn ownership becomes unclear. |
| Device / locale | Actual touch, text entry, IME, reading and layout on declared hardware | Ask a fluent reviewer about meaning and tone, not just missing strings | Rendered text passes but intention is unclear; targets or keyboard hide necessary content. |

Retain failures and disagreements. A single success or enthusiastic quote is a direction for the next test, not a proof that the redesigned game is fun.
