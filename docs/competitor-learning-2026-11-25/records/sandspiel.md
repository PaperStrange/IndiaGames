## Criticism from different roles

| Perspective | What this attempt supports | What to challenge |
|---|---|---|
| Player | Each mark leaves a visible, revisable consequence; the draining basin creates a follow-up question. | A player without an experiment in mind may find a blank objective. My preassigned task cannot demonstrate that the opening solves motivation. |
| Systems designer | Placement, material and erasure alter a persistent situation; one input verb supports several decisions. | Adding 20 materials is not automatically depth. My first overlapping construction obscured cause and effect; legibility and recovery matter. |
| Narrative designer | A sequence of creation → disruption → intervention can produce an authored-by-action incident without a dialogue choice. | It does not establish attachment to a named character. Our character stories still need a meaningful stake, not merely a simulation behind a portrait. |
| Art / interaction | Palette colors and distinct particle arrangements let a still frame communicate water, sand, wall and fire. | Sparse icons and fine strokes need separate accessibility/device review. Low-FPS observation cannot establish tactile pleasure or animation quality. |
| Operator | Actual browser entry required no purchase or account during this attempt. | External resources and slow rendering affect the journey. Loading, device performance, save/publish and recovery must be checked separately from homepage availability. |

## Transfer to IndiaGames — hypotheses, not selected implementation

**Notebook:** preserve the player's own mark, then let shape, position, connection or erasure change a persistent state. Compare a material scene, a constructive route and a restoration task before selecting a core. Do not reduce ink to a key that opens an otherwise unchanged paragraph; do not assume a material sandbox alone is market differentiation.

**Vivid:** a drawn/aimed intervention should change something observable about the person or world and leave room for correction. The test is whether a fresh player can explain the consequence and form a next intention; more motion alone is insufficient.

**Bell:** contrast a stated cause with a visibly changed outcome; expose why a reversal worked. **Small Hours:** this solo creation does not establish cooperation. A shared drawing must let the second person's contribution materially affect the first and must be tested by an actual pair.

Next validation should compare an unprompted start with one optional small invitation, record the first self-chosen action and its explanation, then offer a genuine stop. No time-to-action or continuation rate is claimed from this automated attempt.
