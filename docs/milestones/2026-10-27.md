## The selected Bell experiment

The crowd blocks the main alley at the ordinary Dawn→Noon boundary, regardless of correct, wrong or blank forecast. A committed Square action crossing that boundary witnesses Mina and her crate stranded at the auction stalls and records one clue with event, loop, phase and place provenance. Opening/cancelling an encounter, hearing a report elsewhere or arriving afterward does not grant eyewitness knowledge.

On a later loop, the clue enables preparing the side passage before Noon. Ordinary preparation costs one phase and one Fracture. An eligible borrowed visit costs one Momentum and one Fracture, preserves Dawn and keeps the forecast locked until a real boundary. The prepared route sends Mina and the crate to the sheltered market well, visible in scene, map and journal. No Momentum still permits the ordinary action.

Commitments, spends, boundaries and rewards resolve once. Fracture reaching four before Noon interrupts the pending event and grants no reward for a skipped forecast event; previously resolved outcomes remain recorded. The clue survives midnight, daily preparation and borrowing reset, full restart clears knowledge, modes isolate runs and reload continues to reset the session. Late, missing-clue, missed and already-prepared paths explain their actual limit. Existing anchors and the complete ending remain test targets.
