## User outcome

As a **handwriting user**, I want **more accurate recognition with editable local handwriting reveal**, so that **I spend less effort correcting the app**.

## Why this work is needed

Actual OCR executes, but representative accuracy and alternatives are not benchmarked.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] **AC1.** Given 300 consented annotations from 20 writers, when evaluated on a writer-separated holdout, then English targets are CER at most 8%, WER at most 12% and at least 20% relative CER improvement over TrOCR.
- [ ] **AC2.** Given names, negation and numerals, when scored, then intent preservation targets at least 90% and category failures remain visible.
- [ ] **AC3.** Given any interpretation result, when shown, then original ink is preserved and correction/fade semantics remain intact; no unverified image-model dependency is assumed.

## Implementation outline

1. Collect permitted examples and define reference transcriptions.
2. Compare vision/recognition adapters using the same holdout.
3. Render editable text locally and evaluate latency/cost.

## Scope and readiness

Implement this bounded outcome; preserve the accepted ink interaction and existing functionality.

**Readiness:** Consent, reviewer time and verified live model configuration needed.

## Required review artifacts

- Writer split and consent
- Correction/latency comparison
