## User outcome

As a **notebook owner**, I want **restorable synced content and attachments**, so that **a server incident does not permanently erase my work**.

## Why this work is needed

Source-code backups do not restore customer data, keys and deletion state.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] **AC1.** Given a controlled backup and operation log, when restored into an isolated environment twice, then notes, attachment references and ownership reconcile.
- [ ] **AC2.** Given deletion records and revoked access, when restoring, then they are applied before the data becomes accessible.
- [ ] **AC3.** Given timed drills, when reporting recovery, then target RPO at most 15 minutes and RTO at most four hours are measured; unsynced-only device loss is explicitly excluded.

## Implementation outline

1. Define encrypted backup/key recovery and retention.
2. Restore objects, records and tombstones together.
3. Time drills and record failures/remediation.
