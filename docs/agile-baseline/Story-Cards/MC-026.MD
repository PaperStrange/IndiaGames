## User outcome

As a **founder**, I want **attributable partner-led acquisition**, so that **growth does not depend on one promotional post**.

## Why this work is needed

No repeatable channel or exclusive distribution is verified.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] **AC1.** Given authorized outreach to ten potential partners, when responses are recorded, then acceptance, refusal, time and costs remain visible.
- [ ] **AC2.** Given three independent permissioned placements, when tracked, then visits, activation and real payments are attributable; target 20 attributed payers is a proposed learning gate.
- [ ] **AC3.** Given removal of the largest channel, when economics are recalculated, then concentration risk is explicit and partner repeat interest is recorded.

## Implementation outline

1. Define useful original templates/learning ritual and partner fit.
2. Prepare outreach drafts for authorized sending.
3. Track cohort quality and repeat placements, not just clicks.
