## User outcome

As a **product team**, I want **observations of actual interrupted learning workflows**, so that **we choose a segment based on behavior rather than demo compliments**.

## Why this work is needed

The learner segment and willingness to pay remain hypotheses.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] **AC1.** Given consenting adult candidates, when five interviews are completed or recruitment gaps logged, then notes describe recent actual tasks, tools, friction and alternatives without leading with our UI.
- [ ] **AC2.** Given research notes, when synthesized, then observations, interpretations and hypotheses are separated; private journal content is excluded unless separately consented.
- [ ] **AC3.** Given conflicting evidence, when selecting the pilot segment, then the decision log records why the segment remains or changes.

## Implementation outline

1. Recruit via permissioned channels; do not send outreach without authorization.
2. Observe a recent interruption and how context is recovered.
3. Summarize jobs, switching anxiety and disconfirming evidence.

## Scope and readiness

Implement this bounded outcome; preserve the accepted ink interaction and existing functionality.

**Readiness:** Recruitment and research consent; budget two product/research days, not zero total effort.

## Required review artifacts

- Consent record
- Anonymized interview synthesis
- Segment decision

## Definition of done and evidence

Acceptance criteria pass on the candidate revision; changed behavior has meaningful tests and relevant UI checks; critical privacy/data-loss defects are closed. Record the source/deployment, test environment and limitations. A fixture does not count as a live provider test, and a mobile bundle does not count as physical-device evidence.
