## User outcome

As a **returning learner**, I want **recall linked to its supporting source and version**, so that **I can verify what the companion says about my notes**.

## Why this work is needed

Current lexical excerpts and page links lack complete versioned source integrity.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] **AC1.** Given an answer citing a note, when I open the citation, then the correct source/block and cited version or explicit historical view is shown.
- [ ] **AC2.** Given insufficient support, when answering, then the companion acknowledges uncertainty without inventing a citation.
- [ ] **AC3.** Given 100 answerable probes, when evaluated, then correct recall is at least 90% and source support at least 95%, with the denominator and failures retained.

## Implementation outline

1. Add stable source/block versions and locator resolution.
2. Normalize citation provenance across recall and tools.
3. Build held-out source-support probes.
