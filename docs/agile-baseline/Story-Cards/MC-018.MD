## User outcome

As a **notebook user**, I want **replies that handle uncertainty and harmful content responsibly**, so that **I am not misled or insulted while using my personal notes**.

## Why this work is needed

Prompt-level instructions do not establish complete output screening.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] **AC1.** Given a reply stream, when screening detects prohibited severe content, then it is withheld before reveal and the user’s original writing is retained.
- [ ] **AC2.** Given unsupported current-news or personal-memory claims, when replying, then uncertainty or missing evidence is explicit rather than invented certainty.
- [ ] **AC3.** Given a balanced 300-case set across defined risk categories and language slices, when reviewed, then zero critical failures, at least 95% expected handling and at most 5% benign false blocks are targeted; all denominators are reported.

## Implementation outline

1. Define content taxonomy and contextual handling with trust owner.
2. Implement pre-reveal screening/grounding and fallbacks.
3. Evaluate quoted sensitive text, injection and benign cases as well as harmful outputs.

## Scope and readiness

Implement this bounded outcome; preserve the accepted ink interaction and existing functionality.

**Readiness:** Product/trust policy and language-qualified reviewers needed; proposed thresholds are finite-test gates, not guarantees.
