## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] **AC1.** Given 24 consenting target users, when tasks are counterbalanced with comparable models and sources, then setup/correction time, useful continuation, accuracy and preference are recorded.
- [ ] **AC2.** Given the proposed gate, when results are assessed, then at least 16/24 preference and 20% median time improvement without worse accuracy are used as directional criteria, with uncertainty disclosed.
- [ ] **AC3.** Given an equivalent-memory export and a plain visual treatment, when alternatives are tested, then migration friction and aesthetics are distinguished from lasting workflow value.

## Implementation outline
