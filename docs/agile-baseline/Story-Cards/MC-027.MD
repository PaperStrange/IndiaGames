## User outcome

As a **founder**, I want **cohort retention and real payment evidence**, so that **we do not scale an attractive but uneconomic demo**.

## Why this work is needed

No paid retention, actual CAC or delivery-cost economics are established.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] **AC1.** Given fully observed activated cohorts, when days 22–28 are measured, then meaningful return target is at least 30%; first-session activation target is 70% and usefulness ratings 70% with coverage disclosed.
- [ ] **AC2.** Given a real priced offer, when a pilot runs, then at least ten actual payers is a learning target, not proof of product-market fit.
- [ ] **AC3.** Given at least 100 payers and three months of cohorts, when considering scaling, then proposed gates are monthly paid churn trending at most 5%, contribution margin at least 65% of revenue after channel deductions, and CAC payback at most six months; costs and uncertainty are explicit.

## Implementation outline

1. Define activation, useful return and billing/refund metrics.
2. Use bounded allowances based on measured AI/tool costs.
3. Analyze complete cohorts and stress-test prices/churn.

## Scope and readiness

Implement this bounded outcome; preserve the accepted ink interaction and existing functionality.

**Readiness:** Requires live billing/offer setup and sufficiently observed cohorts; do not forecast observed results.

## Required review artifacts

- Paid cohort dashboard
- Unit economics with assumptions
- Scale/hold decision

## Definition of done and evidence

Acceptance criteria pass on the candidate revision; changed behavior has meaningful tests and relevant UI checks; critical privacy/data-loss defects are closed. Record the source/deployment, test environment and limitations. A fixture does not count as a live provider test, and a mobile bundle does not count as physical-device evidence.
