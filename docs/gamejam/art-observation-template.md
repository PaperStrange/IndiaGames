## Three evidence strips

1. **Clue → theory → action → confirmation.** Pair the original visible clue with a theory recorded before feedback. Include a failed chain if one occurred. Do not retrospectively invent a deduction.
2. **Before → acknowledgment → settled result.** Reference the unedited clip or timestamped sequence for timing. Still images show composition and state, not animation quality by themselves.
3. **First appearance → later reinterpretation.** Identify what changed in meaning or possibility, and what merely looks different.
