# Six-person formative comparison — preregistration instructions

Status: prepared, not registered with participants and not run. No human results exist in this packet. Freeze the candidate and reference identities from their manifests before recruitment. Do not silently change either artifact mid-study.

## Before any session

An actual facilitator obtains consent and privately keeps contact/consent mappings. Register the first six session IDs, distinct participant IDs, UTC registration time, exact candidate and reference hashes, hardware/browser and build order before starting. Keep failures, exits and no-shows visible; do not replace unfavorable attempts. Use three reference-first and three candidate-first assignments, alternating in registration order. Recruit fresh players; record genre familiarity and any previous exposure. No outreach is performed by this implementation.

Serve the reference and candidate from separate local HTTP paths/origins in a browser. The old reference includes Original and previous Contrast: use **Contrast for comparison**, preserving Original for inspection only. Use a fresh page for each run, select Contrast, then Start. Explain only: "Explore the town and try to understand what your actions change. You can stop at any time." Let the player choose their route. No pointing to Square, correct forecast, clue, preparation or borrowing. No solution demonstration. Record any help verbatim and mark the relevant outcome assisted.

## During each run

Capture entry, expectation, first action, encounter, forecast, return, intervention/missed opportunity, outcome and exit. At a naturally chosen decision, ask neutrally: "What do you expect to happen, and why?" Record the answer **before** the outcome without correcting it. Distinguish actions and quotes from your interpretation. Ask what the action costs; do not supply "Fracture" or a correct explanation. Record rereads, wrong forecasts, cancellations, requests for help, confusion and optional retry/leave.

If the participant stops or cannot progress, keep the run as incomplete. Use a **20-minute maximum per build**, allowing earlier voluntary stopping; a limit is not a completion time. On ending each build, record end time. Conduct at least **120 seconds** of neutral unrelated activity with no game screen, discussion or feedback. Then ask "What changed in the town because of something you did?" and record exact recall time and answer. Finish recall before exposing the other build. This is short-delay recall, not retention.

## Predeclared decision

- At least 4/6 candidate players **each both** explain the witnessed cause and predict the consequence before the result.
- At least 4/6 **each both** perform the earned action without coaching and explain that Fracture can still end the day.
- At least 3/6 recall Mina's specific changed outcome after the neutral break.
- Zero repeatable wrong-state, duplicate-reward or inaccessible-action failures. A critical bug blocks the next session until repaired and re-frozen; retain earlier failed records and start a reviewed protocol for changed bytes.

Report all six plus the two first-build-order groups of three separately. Keep reference results separate from candidate release metrics. Learning in the second build is a confound, not proof of superiority. If successful actions rely on cues without causal explanation, revise clarity; if recall is only a meter, revise the payoff; incomplete recruitment leaves the decision pending. Immediate retries are not delayed return.
