## Three answers to test

| Question | Working answer and supporting observation | What our pilot must challenge |
|---|---|---|
| **Why might people want to play?** | A short, intelligible act can give curiosity somewhere to go: help someone, discover an intention, leave a place changed. The funded-jester payoff, Anxiety's acknowledgement and Townscaper's revised composition make earlier action visible later. Galatea's bounded authored topics also give a character consistent agency without unlimited chat. | Which precise action makes the player want to continue, if any? Does a person care about the consequence once the presentation novelty passes? Capture their words and behavior instead of assuming enjoyment from completion. |
| **How could this stand apart on a browser platform?** | Offer a small anthology whose actions fit its stories, with clear entry promises, safe retries, local memory and complete alternatives to dragging/3D. Short play, parallax and attractive art already exist among competitors; they are supporting features rather than a proven distinction. | Can a fresh player describe this specific experience and use its controls without coaching? Do story-specific verbs feel meaningful rather than interchangeable chores? Portal acquisition, commercial conversion and broad preference remain untested. |
| **What unusually specific experience could we deliver?** | Iona: notice a worn place, perform its earlier act of care, then place a personally meaningful object there on return—sheltered letter or approved public dedication. Mara: inspect fragments in any order and align material evidence until a station becomes a possible workshop, then choose an ungraded interpretation. | Can the same unassisted person explain their action and visible consequence? Does Iona's return feel connected to the actual choice, and does Mara's manipulation carry story meaning? No worldwide originality claim is established. |

The [existing synthesis](../vivid-iteration-2026-10-21/research/SYNTHESIS.md) supplies the detailed action-to-design trail and intended journey. Edith Finch informs the narrative method through attributed developer accounts, not an invented playthrough here. The current architecture already supports replaceable cast/content, pure story reducers, independent saves and scene-owned labels. A weak human result should first revise the relevant story/interaction; it does not justify adding services, unrestricted dialogue or more story packs by default.
