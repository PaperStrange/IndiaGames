# The Thirteenth Bell — a route remembered

Milestone: one test-ready causal scene, not a human-approved release. The settled story is **Prepare Mina's route**. An agent may check the game's state and controls; only real players can tell us whether the experience feels earned.

## Three design questions

| Question | Working answer | Evidence and next test |
|---|---|---|
| Why play? | To turn understanding of a strange town into a useful action for a named person. | Anchorhead's learned name opened access; Seedship made outcomes about people. These agent records suggest a mechanism, not proven demand. Ask players to predict before the result. |
| How do we differ in a browser setting? | A small, legible loop lets one witnessed event unlock a timely intervention, with a real opportunity cost and a visible destination. | Desktop Dungeons made a local cost readable. Bell should put Fracture and time beside the action. No claim of market uniqueness or portal acceptance. |
| What distinctive experience can we deliver? | "I remembered why Mina was trapped, prepared another path, and found her and the crate at the well." | Compare current Contrast with the new candidate; ask for the specific outcome after two neutral minutes. A remembered meter alone is insufficient. |

## Intended player journey

| Beat | Intended action and visible result | What an observer records |
|---|---|---|
| Arrival | Choose Contrast, inspect the forecast and Square | Expectations, entry time, first self-chosen action |
| First boundary | Commit an action in Square at Dawn; see the crowd block Mina's alley at Noon | What was actually seen versus inferred or reported |
| Remember | Carry the sourced clue through midnight | Retrieval, rereading and confusion; no facilitator solution |
| Return | Before Noon, prepare the side passage normally or spend an eligible borrowed minute | Prediction before result, resource understanding, help |
| Consequence | At the boundary, see Mina stranded or at the sheltered market well | Explanation in the player's own words, observable reaction |
| Reflection | Neutral two-minute break, unaided outcome recall, then retry/leave | Specific Mina outcome, missing recall, voluntary immediate choice |

This is the intended journey. Actual automated runs belong in `verification.md`; human observations stay blank until conducted.

## Scene and state contract

One Square Dawn→Noon event resolves regardless of correct/wrong/blank forecast. A committed Square action that crosses the boundary earns an eyewitness clue; reports elsewhere and late visits do not. On a later loop, the clue enables preparation before Noon. Ordinary preparation costs one phase and one Fracture; an eligible borrowed visit spends one Momentum, adds one Fracture, holds the phase and leaves the forecast unresolved. The next normal boundary resolves once.

Fracture four before Noon interrupts the event, with no fabricated Mina outcome or reward for a skipped forecast. Already resolved outcomes remain history. Clue knowledge survives midnight; daily preparation/outcome/borrowing resets. Full restart clears knowledge. Modes remain isolated and reload resets the session. The old Original and Contrast builds remain together in the immutable [reference](reference/index.html), with identity in `reference/manifest.json`.
