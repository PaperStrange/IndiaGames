Before the first pass, declare source, critic, solution and teammate knowledge. Log the precise boundary if new outside knowledge is encountered. Note whether predictions were written before acting or reconstructed afterward. Do not use hidden state to choose novice actions.

## Promise and arrival

Record the store/creator's promise as an attributed claim. What did the evaluator expect? What context might bring a player here? Distinguish that audience hypothesis from an actual participant's reason for arriving. Capture barriers to entry without generalizing cloud/tool failures to normal consumer access.

Choose two or three revealing episodes. Explain anticipation, choice, uncertainty, discovery, payoff and friction. Explain who might value the sequence and under what circumstances. Preserve a contrary episode. Do not manufacture an emotional curve or an overall human enjoyment score.

## Systems map

Describe the player verb → information → decision → feedback → changed future possibilities. Record resources and pressure, reversibility, failure costs, persistence, and one observed interaction between systems. Separate rules observed directly from rules inferred or later found in source.

## Social-session addendum, when applicable

Record actual participant count and relevant context without identifying details; who explained, contributed, waited, guessed and declined. Trace join → private work → public reveal → results → rematch/exit. Separate controller screens from shared screen. Keep each participant's account, including disagreement. An agent playing several roles can produce only a labeled mechanical rehearsal.
