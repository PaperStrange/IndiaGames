## What the coverage says about Small Hours

Nothing in this round changes the October 28 reading: the crowded family is draw-and-guess (Gartic, skribbl) — Small Hours does not compete there; it competes on the keepsake and the pair. The empty family is the one Small Hours owns: a 5-minute, one-screen, two-person memory-maker with a downloadable artifact. No competitor in the six makes a keepsake the pair owns.
