# Small Hours 0.2.0 — demo records, 2026-11-17

This is the November 17 play package for Small Hours 0.2.0: first-hand agent plays in three locales, written in the October 28 record shape. **Vivid 0.2.0 was not played** — no public URL exists for its built dist, and playing it needs a preview-branch push that awaits the owner's word. The 20 November launch date has passed with the human gate unmet.

The labels are `[REAL]` for frame/text-supported states, `[SECONDARY]` for reported or desk material as defined in each record, and `[OPEN]` for unfinished questions. **`[REAL]` does not mean a human playtest or proof of fun.** Distinguish what the frame shows, what a browser-task report says, and what the author interprets. Unsuccessful and contradictory experiences remain preserved. Some filenames differ from content; captions describe the frame rather than treating its filename as proof.

## Still open

- Six-pair human gate, genuine two-person play, physical-device checks and independent human release review. No human results are added here.
- Solo rehearsal, 45-second timer, mismatched-guess reveal: not observed in this round.
- Human motivation, contribution balance, demand and distinctiveness. An SVG download proves a file was delivered, not that a pair wants to keep it.

## Filing and provenance

The five records, the critique, the review page and the raw evidence are filed in this branch. Play scripts and detailed execution logs are intentionally local only. The raw PDFs sit behind the records and page renders, under `evidence/moments/pdf/<task-uuid>/` with their original names. Their addition does not add play sessions: several captures belong to one existing task.

This package replaces the first 2026-11-17 version, which the owner deleted from Drive on 2026-11-24 for two form mistakes: the records paraphrased the template instead of following the October 28 filled-record shape, and the folders put the two own games under `evidence/competitors/`. The play evidence is unchanged — the same captures, the same SVGs, the same verified facts; no re-play was needed. See `notes/rectification-docs-2026-11-24.md`.
