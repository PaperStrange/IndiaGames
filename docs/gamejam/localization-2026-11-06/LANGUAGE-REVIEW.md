Draft terminology is consistent across UI and story catalogs: JA `亀裂` / `機運` / `ループ` / `定着した記憶`; Chinese `裂痕` / `势能` / `循环` / `锚定的记忆`. Character and location IDs never change with translated names. Please review those terms for clarity and tone; consistency alone is not fluency.

One inherited Original-mode narrative inconsistency is deliberately preserved for a separate design decision: the third-loop Square action “Trade your fading satchel” says possessions dissolve, while the existing effect anchors the route without removing items. The distinct Contrast action explicitly says possessions remain. This iteration preserves both historical mechanics and their separate source copy; do not merge the two actions or claim that translation resolved the discrepancy. A reviewed correction would produce new bytes and needs appropriate retesting.
