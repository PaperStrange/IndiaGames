# Access check — Rhythm Doctor (Vivid competitor V6)
**Filed:** 2026-10-20 · **Source:** live browser check of the developer's official pages
**Question:** does an official free browser demo exist? (The game began as a free Flash web demo.)

**Verdict: NO — the browser demo is dead.** Verified 2026-10-20:
- The historical free demo page (fizzd.itch.io/rhythm-doctor-flash, by the developer, 7th Beat Games) states in the developer's own words that the free demo no longer works because browsers no longer support Flash; the embed renders blank. The only free option on that page is a 53 MB offline download — a download, not browser play.
- The main game (fizzd.itch.io/rhythm-doctor) is a paid download: **$20.00 USD minimum**, Windows/macOS/Linux zips (~405–420 MB). No browser option.
- rhythmdoctor.com is now a parked domain ("This Page Is Under Construction"). rhythmdr.com is the genuine official site but carries only store links for the paid release — no demo, no web play.
- Unofficial portal re-uploads exist but were not used (standing rule: official sources only).

**Stop point:** level 0 — the game never ran. No judgment of gameplay is offered.
**What would unblock it:** a paid copy ($20, Sakura's call) or an official HTML5 demo build (none published). The V6 player slot is therefore filled by the family substitute, Friday Night Funkin' (free official browser rhythm game), with this access result kept on the record. Rhythm Doctor's storefront/trailer market evidence is collected under the paid-slot market-records pass.
