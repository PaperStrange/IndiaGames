## The session
The demo opens not on an empty grid but with a small pre-built starter town (a yellow house with red pitched roof beside a red tower, trees, railings). From there I built six structures: a red house, a two-story blue tower with an orange conical roof, a two-story green tower with a red spire roof, a yellow-railed platform, and two small stair/railing platforms that merged into the main island — using the red, blue, green, yellow, and white palette colors, stacking to two stories.

Building feels like playing with a kind toy: one click on water raises a stone platform, one more raises a house, another adds a floor — and the game invents all the details itself (windows, doors, chimneys, pitched vs. conical roofs, railings, bushes). No winning, no losing, no instructions, no UI beyond the palette and a settings gear.

Surprises: (1) the whole town state is encoded live in the URL fragment — every edit changed the hash, so the URL is the save file; (2) the game auto-built a staircase connecting my yellow platform to the taller blue tower, unasked; (3) twice, clicking a fresh empty platform removed it instead of raising a house (demolish behavior), and I rebuilt those blocks; (4) the camera slowly auto-rotates — pretty, but it makes precise clicking finicky.
