## Commercial facts (as written on the storefront, 2026-10-20)
- **itch.io price:** $18.00 USD minimum ("Buy Now $18.00 USD or more") for the full game: Windows 114 MB, macOS 128 MB, Android 66 MB.
- **Demo:** free downloadable builds for the same three platforms (same file sizes). No content/time limit stated. **No browser/HTML5 build exists** — see the access-check record.
- **Author's own pricing note (paraphrase):** itch is deliberately priced at double the other stores because the itch purchase bundles desktop + Android; Android-only is $9 on Google Play; the author directs price-sensitive buyers to Google Play, Steam, or iOS.
- **Platforms:** Windows, macOS, Android (itch); also Steam and iOS per the official site. Status: Released.
- **Traction:** rated 4.9 out of 5 stars across **768 ratings** on itch; 705 comments. Genre: Strategy, Role Playing. Made with libGDX. Average session: "About a half-hour." Inputs: mouse. Languages: English, plus FR/IT/RU/ES/PT translations added May 2025.
- **Maintenance cadence (dev log):** 2.0 (Oct 2022) → 3.0 (Mar 2024) → 3.1 (Nov 2024) → 3.2 (May 12, 2025) → 3.2.13 (May 21, 2025). Actively maintained three years after 2.0.
- **Official-site positioning bullets:** "free demo, single purchase" · "no ads, mtx, data" · "rating ~4.8/5" · "offline, low battery" · "turn-based with undo."

## Market read (labeled interpretation)
A premium, one-time-purchase roguelite that markets the *absence* of monetization mechanics ("no ads, mtx, data") as a headline feature, sustains a 4.9-star rating at meaningful volume (768 ratings), and is still receiving content updates three years on. Channel pricing is deliberate: the itch bundle (desktop + Android in one purchase) is priced at 2× the single-platform stores, with the author openly explaining why — an unusually transparent storefront posture. For the Bell comparison, its competitive weight is in *session shape*: ~30-minute turn-based runs with an undo — knowledge-and-planning satisfaction in a compact, replayable loop.
