# Recruitment and screening drafts

**Unsent preparation.** No invitations have been sent and no people are enrolled. A human coordinator chooses the contact channel, compensation if any, consent process and secure evidence location before outreach. These documents do not authorize an agent to contact anyone.

## Invitation draft

“We are looking for adults to try a short browser story prototype and tell us what happens as they play. A session takes about 20–25 minutes. You do not need to be good at games; unclear instructions and mistakes are useful feedback about the prototype. You may stop at any time. We will ask for permission before recording anything. The game uses authored text and illustrations. Please do not look up the prototype or a walkthrough before the session. Reply only if you would like the coordinator to send the details.”

Insert the real coordinator, scheduling information, compensation and consent terms before using this draft. Do not imply a live public release, an existing participant pool, proven fun or known benefits. Do not name the mystery solution, the Iona choice, the return mechanic or the condition a person will receive in recruitment material.

## Screening fields, held privately

- Adult age eligibility, willingness and informed consent. Do not recruit minors under this preparation protocol.
- Has the person played, watched, tested, developed or read a walkthrough of this candidate or either story? If yes, do not put them in a fresh cohort; they may participate later in a separately labeled expert review.
- General browser-game familiarity: rarely, sometimes or often. This describes the sample; it is not a score of suitability or predicted enjoyment.
- Available computer/phone, operating system, browser and normal input method. Ask whether keyboard navigation, reduced motion, larger text, screen-reader support or another accommodation is needed. Do not require disclosure of a diagnosis.
- Availability for a 20–25-minute session and, only if preregistered, the optional 48–72-hour recall contact. Declining recall must not exclude an otherwise eligible person from the main session.
- Consent to concise deidentified notes, optional recording, and retention/deletion terms supplied by the human coordinator. Keep contact information separate from observation IDs.

## Allocation and diversity targets

Recruit toward six remembered-Iona, six neutral-Iona and six Mara sessions, using separate people. Before first contact is assigned, write an alternating allocation sequence across the intended cohorts. Assign each next eligible recruit to the next slot; do not assign someone to a condition because of a predicted reaction. If only the two required cohorts are feasible, declare that before testing and omit all comparative claims.

Aim across the full pilot for both infrequent and frequent game players, at least two people using keyboard as their primary input, and at least two sessions on actual physical phones. These are coverage targets, not substitutes for six valid sessions per required story or evidence that every supported device is tested. Record missed targets. Accessibility and physical-device observations may reveal issues even if the participant otherwise completes the narrative.

Use participant pseudonyms unrelated to names or contact details. A person cannot appear in more than one cohort, serve as an observer, or approve the release. The independent reviewer must be outside all participant and observer roles. Do not fill session slots with project agents or generated personas.
