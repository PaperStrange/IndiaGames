# What the browser play changes about Vivid

21 October 2026. These are design conclusions from agent-operated sessions and attributed criticism, not population findings. Read the [edition and endpoint ledger](README.md) before using them. A completed bounded task, a partial game and an assisted attempt carry different evidence. There are no human enjoyment, retention or purchase-intent results.

## The three product questions

| Question | Evidence from actual play | Vivid's testable answer |
|---|---|---|
| **Why might someone want to play?** | Sort the Court made a funded jester's show return as a named later event. Townscaper turned a few clicks into an authored blue annex and pier, including an unexpected arch. Anxiety later recalled earlier conduct and required an acknowledgement of harm. | Curiosity can lead to a manageable act with a legible consequence: help a person, discover an intention or leave a small place changed. Offer the player something specific to do and understand. This is a motivation hypothesis, not a measured preference. |
| **How can it stand apart on a browser-game platform?** | The compared games already offer short interactions, strong art direction, constrained inputs, narrative consequences or miniature making. Sort the Court's Poki edition also places the game among portal recommendations and optional rewards. Six Cats exposed uncertainty about a small target; ADOFAI required precise timing even with one key. | Present a small anthology with a clear promise at entry, local assets, separate story verbs, safe retries and complete alternatives to dragging or 3D. Spend interaction complexity on the story rather than on discovering how to operate a tiny target. Browser access, parallax, few buttons and pleasant art are insufficient differentiators by themselves. Portal acquisition, conversion and monetization remain untested. |
| **What unusually specific experience could we deliver?** | Delayed recognition in Sort the Court and Anxiety makes past conduct relevant again. Townscaper gives authorship through arrangement. WBWWB's photographed subjects visibly change the shared scene, demonstrating how framing can carry meaning. | In Iona's story, the player first notices a worn place, performs its earlier act of care, then places a personally meaningful object there on return. Privacy leaves a sheltered folded letter; approved public recognition leaves a dedication. Mara uses a different structure: arrange material evidence until an empty station becomes a possible workshop. The candidate combines these ideas; no worldwide originality claim has been established. |

## Decisions tied to observed moments

| Observed moment | Decision in the implemented candidate | Human observation that could overturn it |
|---|---|---|
| The jester's later show explicitly names the earlier investment; some other court rulings reveal costs only afterward. | Iona's return has a concrete object and place. Explain permission before the new public dedication; preserve the old archive's actual publication history. | A player cannot explain what was shared or why the object appeared. |
| Anxiety's later confrontation refers to repeated false alarms, and apology acknowledges the earlier harm. | Keep premature-reading acknowledgement and truthful legacy migration. Do not erase an inconvenient prior choice to make the ending warmer. | Players read acknowledgement as punishment, coercion or a contradiction of the actual action. |
| Townscaper's joined roofs and railings respond immediately; revision can become part of authorship. | Let valid placement and sketch alignment change the visible scene immediately. Give invalid placement a safe recovery and keep Mara's alignment reversible. | People cannot distinguish a preview from an accepted change, or a wrong drop feels like lost progress. |
| Six Cats' indirect object chain made progress through cat preferences; a small remote interaction remained unresolved in the agent attempt. | Use readable named controls alongside the illustrated objects. No story-critical action depends solely on pixel hunting, camera movement or a drag. | Fresh players still ask where to click, miss the alternative or accidentally trigger a different action. |
| ADOFAI's actual tutorial attempt exposed a timing error and retry after a menu with only a few controls. Audio was not heard. | Keep Vivid's care and discovery actions untimed, without sustained holds or simultaneous inputs. | A player finds the actions mechanically empty despite understanding them; adding speed is not the default remedy. |
| WBWWB visibly turns a selected frame into a headline and changes what appears in the crowd. | Judge narrative mechanics by their visible consequence, not the quantity of dialogue choices. A story pack owns its state, cast, scene captions and accessible labels. | Mara feels like an unrelated puzzle, or a new story needs Iona-specific conditions in the shared engine. |

These observations refine the implemented hypotheses; they do not retroactively prove that every design choice originated from the later follow-up play. [The accepted decision](../DECISION.md) records the earlier implementation direction. The [source and criticism audit](../reference/README.md) distinguishes the linked creator's reported Tripo/Blender/Unity workflow from our Three.js browser adaptation. Edith Finch informs the method through developer accounts; it was not played in this environment.

Same-session return and prompted 48–72-hour recall are not organic retention. Agent sessions cannot fill these rows.

## Limits and next research work

The roster covers six titles and five broad families, but its records include assisted and partial journeys. Finish unresolved puzzle/track/story endpoints in a better-suited input environment before calling those playthroughs complete. Preserve failed attempts and assistance, then add new sessions rather than rewriting them. Native Event[0], TOEM, full Townscaper and Rhythm Doctor remain gaps. No physical phone, audible rhythm assessment, screen-reader study or commercial platform experiment was completed.

Keep comparative claims narrow. The immediate development decision is to test whether each Vivid action communicates its particular story, and whether remembered consequences add value over the neutral return. A human finding that the actions feel arbitrary, unclear or interchangeable should lead to revising the relevant story, not adding more spectacle or content by default.
