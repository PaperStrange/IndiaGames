## Questions these readings sharpen

- Can a player explain why the notch, wedge and lamp belong in Iona's particular story, rather than seeing them as an unrelated minigame?
- Does Mara's lightbox make interpretation visible without requiring an emotionally prescribed answer?
- Does a returning player recognise their own earlier choice from the placed object before the text explains it?
- Can someone complete every meaningful action without a timed input, a drag, audio, or 3D rendering?

These questions belong in human observation sheets. Agent play and criticism make them more specific; they do not answer them on behalf of human players.
