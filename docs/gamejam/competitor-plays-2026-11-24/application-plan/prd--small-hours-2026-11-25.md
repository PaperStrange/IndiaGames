# PRD — Small Hours: the keepsake must depend on two people

**Status:** proposal, 2026-11-25. Nothing here is a solution until its gate passes.
**Order in the plan:** third. The synthesis's sequence runs the separate game agents after the Vivid comparison (step 5).

## Description

Small Hours is a five-minute, one-screen, two-person shared story ending in a downloadable keepsake. Version 0.2.0 was played end to end in English, Japanese, and Simplified Chinese by one operator playing both sides — ending and keepsake in each locale. Solo rehearsal, the 45-second timer, and the mismatched-guess reveal were not observed. A genuinely shared two-person experience is untested: one operator played both sides.

## Problem

The keepsake does not visibly depend on two people — a solo operator produces a full keepsake — and its differentiator is unproven. Gartic's shareable album is the nearest rival artifact. No pair has tested whether the keepsake feels worth keeping.

## Why now

Counterfoil (shared story — each player holds half the evidence), Jigsaw Together (shared board — two players, one picture, no score), Gartic Phone (shareable album), and Draw a Stickman EP2 (the drawing is the character) supply four distinct models for what "shared" can mean. The six-pair human gate is the only test that can settle the keepsake question, and this PRD's hypotheses are written for that gate.

## Success

The six-pair gate returns an answer: pairs either describe the keepsake as theirs because both made it, or they do not — and the hypotheses are kept or killed on that evidence. A killed hypothesis is a result, not a failure.

## Audience

Pairs of real players — six pairs, the human gate. The Small Hours game agent runs the gate.

## What

**H1 — dependence.** The team believes a keepsake assembled from both players' contributions will be described by pairs as theirs because both made it, and a solo run will produce a visibly incomplete keepsake. Evidence: Counterfoil, where the case closes only when the halves meet [REAL, `records/session--counterfoil-agent-2026-11-24.md`]; Jigsaw Together, where two players share one board [REAL, `records/session--co-op-puzzle-agent-2026-11-24.md`]. Limit, stated plainly: both deep studies were one-operator sessions — Jigsaw's two seats were driven by one person in two tabs — so the shared models are shapes observed in play, not validated pair experiences. Gate: in the six-pair test, at least four of six pairs describe the keepsake as jointly made AND solo runs produce visibly incomplete keepsakes. Kill: if pairs cannot distinguish their keepsake from a solo one, the dependence mechanic is dropped.

**H2 — the keepsake records a decision.** The team believes a keepsake that records a decision the pair made will be pointed at by pairs as the moment they chose. Evidence: Gartic Phone, whose shareable album is the nearest rival artifact [REAL, `records/session--gartic-phone-agent-2026-11-24.md`]; the keepsake must record a choice, not a souvenir. Gate: pairs point at one line on the keepsake and say what they chose there. Kill: if no pair does, the decision-record element is dropped.

**H3 — the contributed object functions.** The team believes an object contributed by a player that changes the next scene will be used deliberately. Evidence: Draw a Stickman EP2, where the drawing is the character and drawn objects (ladder, tiger, wings) function in the scene [REAL, `records/session--draw-a-stickman-agent-2026-11-24.md`]. Gate: players contribute an object to change a scene, not to decorate it. Kill: if objects are used only decoratively, the functional-object element is dropped.

**H4 — retention.** The team believes a daily shared prompt — a new pair decision each day — will bring pairs back tomorrow. Evidence: Codenames Daily (daily set, percentile, countdown) [REAL, `records/session--codenames-agent-2026-11-24.md`]; 2048 (single-number chase) [REAL, `records/session--2048-agent-2026-11-24.md`]. Gate: a pair returns on a second day. Kill: no second-day return. The likely cause, named in advance: the coordination cost — both people must be present at the same time.
