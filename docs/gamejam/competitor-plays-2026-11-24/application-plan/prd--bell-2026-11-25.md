# PRD — The 13th Bell: the re-encounter must change the decision

**Status:** proposal, 2026-11-25. Nothing here is a solution until its gate passes.
**Order in the plan:** fourth. The synthesis's sequence runs the separate game agents after the Vivid comparison (step 5).

## Description

The 13th Bell is a time-loop town game. Version 0.2.0 Contrast mode was played to "Adventure Complete" in 4:35 — 3 loops, 10 visits, Momentum 4, Fracture 4. The advance rule was verified in play at Loop 2 Dawn. Dusk→Midnight was played three ways (normal, catastrophe at Fracture 4, release). Original mode, the Japanese and Simplified Chinese journeys, and rules 3/4/5/7 are untested; the How to play text is byte-identical to 0.1.0.

## Problem

The same town event re-encountered does not yet offer a different decision — the loop changes the text, not the choice. The advance rule is taught on a modal, not where the player learns the game, and its generalization beyond Contrast is untested.

## Why now

Sort the Court shows one verb serving many decision types; Celeste Classic shows onboarding fails when the controls are not taught where the player acts; Townscaper shows hover preview teaching intent. The advance rule already exists in play — the question is whether the loop changes the decision.

## Success

A loop-3 event offers a decision loop 1 did not; the advance rule is taught at the point of action; the rule's generalization is tested in Original mode and in Japanese and Simplified Chinese.

## Audience

The Bell game agent. Fresh uncoached players for the baselines; fluent reviewers for the Japanese and Simplified Chinese surfaces.

## What

**H1 — the re-encounter offers a new decision.** The team believes a town event re-encountered with new causal understanding will offer a decision the first encounter did not, and players will take it. Evidence: the synthesis's distinctive-experience contract for Bell — "the same town event re-encountered with new causal understanding allowing a different outcome" [SECONDARY — synthesis, not a play record, `docs/competitor-learning-2026-11-25/SYNTHESIS.md`]; Sort the Court, where one Y/N verb serves investment, rapport, refusal, and moral cost [REAL, `docs/competitor-learning-2026-11-25/records/sort-court.md`]. Gate: in a fresh baseline, a player takes the new decision at the re-encounter and can say what changed. Kill: if players repeat the same choice at the re-encounter, the new-decision element is dropped.

**H2 — one verb, many costs.** The team believes the same "borrow a minute" choice carrying different costs in different loops will make players weigh the loop, not just the moment. Evidence: Sort the Court, where one verb carries different decision types [REAL, `docs/competitor-learning-2026-11-25/records/sort-court.md`]; Bell's Momentum and Fracture numbers already exist in play — Momentum 4, Fracture 4 in the 0.2.0 Contrast run [REAL, `docs/gamejam/competitor-plays-2026-11-17/records/` on the `the-thirteenth-bell` branch]. Gate: players describe the cost difference between loops. Kill: if players treat every borrow as identical, the varying-cost element is dropped.

**H3 — teach at the action.** The team believes showing a choice's consequence before the player commits — at the choice point — will reduce confusion about the advance rule. Evidence: Celeste Classic, where the page never explains which key dashes or jumps [REAL, `docs/competitor-learning-2026-11-25/records/celeste.md`]; Townscaper, where the hover outline identifies the intended surface [REAL, `docs/competitor-learning-2026-11-25/records/townscaper.md`]; Bell rules 3/4/5/7 unstated in How to play [REAL, Nov-17 Bell records]. Gate: fresh players state the advance rule correctly after one loop without opening How to play. Kill: if players still cannot state it, the teaching location is wrong — revise it; do not add more text. Note the filter: the preview must add a genuine alternative (for example, declining the borrow). A preview that only informs, with the choice set unchanged, is a gesture and fails H3.

**H4 — retention.** The team believes a daily town event — a new decision each day — will bring players back tomorrow. Evidence: Codenames Daily (daily set, percentile, countdown) [REAL, `records/session--codenames-agent-2026-11-24.md`]; 2048 (single-number chase) [REAL, `records/session--2048-agent-2026-11-24.md`]. Gate: a player returns on a second day. Kill: no second-day return.

**Scope note:** H1–H3 are written for Contrast mode. Original mode and the Japanese and Simplified Chinese journeys are separate test surfaces, not assumed covered.
