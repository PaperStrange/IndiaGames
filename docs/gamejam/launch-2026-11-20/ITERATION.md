## Scope and capacity rules

Keep one event, one usable clue and Mina's visible outcome as the release slice. New districts, extra endings, saves/accounts, co-op, procedural cases, generalized simulation, analytics backend and new platforms are deferred. They compete with the human-validation window and are not required to learn whether this scene works.

The six adjusted competitor records retain their edition, substitution and partial-journey limits. A focused follow-up can answer a specific unresolved design question in parallel, but do not substitute more competitor research for the six real Bell players. Market/critic-only and agent observations cannot provide human approval or justify “unique”/“proven fun” launch copy.

At each safe milestone commit and push the owning source/docs, verify the remote SHA and preserve exact candidate identities. Update the team hub separately. Before any shared-checkout branch switch, stop owned servers and inspect changes. If allowance is reported below 5%, stop new work, save completed work, dirty state, unfinished checks and exact restart commands, sync safe completed commits, then wait; the agent cannot see allowance directly.
