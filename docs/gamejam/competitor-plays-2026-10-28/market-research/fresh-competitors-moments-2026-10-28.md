# Fresh competitors for Small Hours (Memorable Moments) — desk-research sweep

**Date:** 2026-10-28 · **Scope:** desk research only (no live-browser play; first-hand validation is a separate session).
**Target:** Small Hours — two people sharing one screen make private choices, reveal them together,
then invent a tiny exhibit / impossible postcard / playful forecast, and may save an SVG keepsake.

**Excluded (already covered historically):** Make it Meme, Fireboy & Watergirl, Twin Flames, Hanabi,
GeoGuessr/HorrorGuessr, Townscaper, Florence, novena, missed messages.
**Correction logged:** *Drawful: Animate* was considered as a 2024 standalone; it is in fact a 2021 game
inside The Jackbox Party Pack 8 — NOT fresh, and not reported as a finding. Its successor pattern is
covered instead by The Jackbox Party Pack 11 (below).

## Per-title detail

### 1. Split Fiction [REAL]
- **Creator / year:** Hazelight Studios, published by EA; released 6 Mar 2025. [REAL — EA press release; Steambase]
- **Platforms / price:** Windows (Steam appid 2001120), PS5, Xbox Series X|S; Epic Games Store and EA app. $49.99 list on the Steam store page [REAL]; 35%-off sale to $32.49 seen in Oct 2026 coverage [SECONDARY]. Friend's Pass: only one player needs to own the game; crossplay enabled. [REAL — EA press release]
- **Reception:** Overwhelmingly Positive, ~125,000 Steam reviews. [REAL — Steambase, citing Steam]
- **Free browser/web demo:** None. [OPEN — no demo on the Steam page text]
- **Design lesson for Small Hours:** Split-screen keeps both players' halves always visible, so neither player ever waits without a stake — Small Hours' two players should each always see their own contribution in play, not take turns watching. [Lesson = analysis]

### 2. PICO PARK 2 [REAL]
- **Creator / year:** Published by TECOPARK; released 12 Sep 2024. [REAL — Steam store text; dev attribution to Gemdrops is [SECONDARY — Steambase]]
- **Platforms / price:** Steam (appid 2644470) on Windows and Mac, plus Nintendo Switch; $8.99 USD. [REAL — Steam store text]
- **Play model:** 2D action-puzzle, 2–8 players locally or online, every level playable at any player count; cross-platform online; up to 7 can join online with a single PC/Switch; smartphone-as-gamepad (TecoGamePad); Steam Deck Verified; Very Positive, 88% of ~4,800 reviews. [REAL — Steam store text]
- **Free browser/web demo:** None found on the Steam page. [OPEN]
- **Design lesson for Small Hours:** Every level works at any player count and a phone can be the controller — for a shared-screen game, low-friction entry (any count, any input) matters more than feature count. [Lesson = analysis]

### 3. BOKURA: planet [REAL]
- **Creator / year:** tokoronyori, published by Kodansha Game Creators' Lab; released 24 Apr 2025. [REAL — Steam community announcement; Gamedeveloper]
- **Platforms / price:** PC via Steam (appid 3126150), Windows; $5.49 USD; BOKURA Series Bundle (both games) $9.88. [REAL — Steam store text]
- **Play model:** Two-player-only "Hidden Objective" puzzle adventure; two players on two machines must communicate (voice chat, not provided in-game); each player secretly holds a different objective, so cooperation and sabotage (new "push" action) coexist; each sees a different world. [REAL — Steam store text and developer announcement]
- **Free browser/web demo:** A standalone demo ran 16 Jan–24 Apr 2025 and ended at launch; no current demo. [SECONDARY — Gamedeveloper/Kodansha announcement]
- **Design lesson for Small Hours:** Hidden objectives make the reveal itself the game — Small Hours' private choices need real weight (a secret worth keeping), or the reveal is a formality. [Lesson = analysis]

### 4. The Jackbox Party Pack 11 [REAL]
- **Creator / year:** Jackbox Games; released 22 Oct 2025 on Steam (press release dated 23 Oct 2025). [REAL — Steam search listing; Gamedeveloper]
- **Platforms / price:** Steam (appid 3364070) on Windows, macOS, SteamOS/Linux; also Xbox, Nintendo Switch, PlayStation, Epic Games Store, Apple TV/iPad, Mac App Store, Amazon Luna. $29.99. [REAL — Steam store page text]
- **Play model:** Five new games, phones/tablets/computers as controllers, no extra hardware: Doominate (writing, 3–8), Hear Say (sound effects, 2–8), Cookie Haus (drawing, 3–8), **Suspectives** (social deduction, 4–8 — one player secretly committed the crime and the evidence comes from the players' own survey answers), Legends of Trivia (1–6). [REAL — Steam store page text]
- **Free browser/web demo:** Yes — "The Jackbox Party Pack 11 Demo", free on Steam since 1 Oct 2025. [REAL — Steam search listing]
- **Design lesson for Small Hours:** Suspectives turns the players' own survey answers into the evidence for the reveal — Small Hours could make each player's private answers the material the other must read and interpret, not just admire. [Lesson = analysis]
- **Note:** The Jackbox Party Pack 12 launched 14 Oct 2026 at the same $29.99 [SECONDARY — Pixel Herald]; Pack 11 is the entry with a free demo and a year of reception.

### 5. Gartic Phone [REAL]
- **Creator / year:** Gartic; launched 2021, still listed among the top free browser party games in Oct 2026 roundups. [Release year SECONDARY; current relevance REAL — 2026 roundups]
- **Platforms / price:** Free in the browser at garticphone.com (no account, invite link, nickname); also Android/iOS; 4–50 players per room (4–10 best); free with ads. [REAL — developer store listing and 2026 roundups]
- **Play model:** Telephone with drawings — write a sentence, next player draws it, next player guesses the drawing; modes include Normal, Knock-Off, Animation, Secret, Background & Solo; albums save/share as animated GIFs and replay stroke-by-stroke. [REAL — developer store listing]
- **Free browser/web demo:** Yes — the game itself is the free browser demo: https://garticphone.com [REAL — FAQ text]
- **Design lesson for Small Hours:** The reveal montage IS the product (albums replayable stroke-by-stroke and exportable as GIFs) — Small Hours' SVG keepsake should be built for replaying and sharing, not just for saving. [Lesson = analysis]

### 6. Tiny Glade [REAL]
- **Creator / year:** Pounce Light; released 23 Sep 2024 (Windows/Linux; macOS 13 May 2026). [REAL — Wikipedia, MobyGames, SteamDB, Steam store text]
- **Platforms / price:** Windows, Linux, macOS; Steam Deck Verified; $14.99 USD; single-player. [REAL — Steam store text]
- **Play model:** Small diorama builder — doodle castles, cottages and ruins with gridless building chemistry and procedural detail; no management, combat or goals; in-game camera/photo modes for the creation. Overwhelmingly Positive, 97% of ~14,000 English reviews (~25,000 total). [REAL — Steam store text]
- **Free browser/web demo:** No browser demo; a Steam demo is available ("Download PC Demo"). [REAL — Steam store page text]
- **Design lesson for Small Hours:** A no-fail, no-goal creative toy with a photo mode proves keepsake value can carry a whole game — but as a single-player title it warns that Small Hours' solo mode must stay honestly labeled rehearsal, never the real thing. [Lesson = analysis]
