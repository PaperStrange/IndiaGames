## Near-misses (checked, not tabled)

- **Slay the Princess — The Pristine Cut** (Black Tabby Games; original 2023, Pristine Cut update Oct 24, 2024 expanded the game 35%+ [REAL]; $17.99 Steam [REAL]): the same cabin/princess premise replayed as branching routes — lesson: name each loop's route so loops stay distinguishable. Visual novel, no investigation cost, no browser demo.
- **The Crimson Diamond** (Julia Minamata; Aug 15, 2024 [REAL]; $14.99 itch/Steam [REAL]): cozy EGA text-parser mystery with a notebook and eavesdropping — lesson: a notebook that records what you heard keeps a slow investigation fair. Its Steam demo was deactivated Aug 12, 2024 [REAL]; no browser demo.
- **Quiet Express: Cabin 909** (STUDIO909; demo on Steam Oct 2026, full game due 2027 [SECONDARY]): a 7-day train-crash loop — find the non-existent cabin to prevent the crash. The closest premise to Bell's "one event, one intervention", but unreleased; watch its demo feedback.

## Synthesis — three transferable lessons for Bell

1. **Fixed routine + one visible change = loop legibility.** Until Then and Blue Prince both keep the repeated day/mansion routine stable so the changed thing is unmissable; Until Then's weak reset signal is the failure mode to avoid.
2. **The carried clue must be persistent AND visible.** ROLLBACK's notebook is Bell's closest mechanic — and its live feedback shows the failure mode: a clue that scrolls away or costs time to re-read stops being a tool.
3. **Preview the cost before the player pays it.** Tactical Breach Wizards shows the outcome, then asks for the commitment; Duck Detective grades a stated prediction. Bell's forecast should be the preview, and Fracture the visible price tag on it — never a blind spend.
