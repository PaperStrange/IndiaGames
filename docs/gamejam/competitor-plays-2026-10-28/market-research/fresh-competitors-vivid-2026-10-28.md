# Fresh competitors for Keeper's Frame (Vivid) — desk-research sweep, 2026-10-28

Scope: fresh comparable titles for Keeper's Frame, a browser 2.5D photo-interaction
narrative game (two short stories: Iona — a performed act of care remembered on a
return visit; Mara — arranging objects to discover a possible future). Desk research
only; no live-browser play in this sweep — first-hand validation is a separate session.

Excluded as already covered (do not re-report): Event[0], TOEM, Townscaper, Rhythm
Doctor, Friday Night Funkin', Sort the Court!, Six Cats Under, Galatea, Beyond the
Trees, We Become What We Behold, A Dance of Fire and Ice.

Evidence labels: [REAL] = read on the store/page text itself; [SECONDARY] = one
other source (review, tracker, wiki); [OPEN] = unverified. Prices are as listed at
crawl time, 2026-10-28, in the currency shown; sale prices are noted, never converted.

## 1. The Star Named EOS — (a) photography narrative

- Creator: Silver Lining Studio; publisher PLAYISM [REAL]
- Release: 22 July 2024 [REAL]
- Platforms: Steam (Windows, Mac; Steam Deck Verified), PS5, Nintendo Switch, Xbox Series X|S, Epic Games Store [REAL]
- Price: $14.99 USD on Steam [REAL]
- Free browser/web demo: No. A free demo exists on Steam (not browser) [REAL]
- Store: https://store.steampowered.com/app/1619230/The_Star_Named_EOS/
- What it is: story-rich puzzle adventure built around photography; the player recreates compositions of old photographs to unlock story elements and uncover a family mystery, in hand-drawn panoramic scenes [REAL]
- Design lesson for Keeper's Frame: a photo is a legible contract — EOS makes the target composition visible before the shot, so the player always knows what "right" looks like; Keeper's Frame should show its remembered-placement goal as plainly before the return visit.

## 2. Birdography — (a) photography narrative

- Creator: Max (notapsycho.itch.io) [REAL]
- Release: 2026; last update 20 October 2026 [REAL for the update date; release year SECONDARY]
- Platforms: HTML5 (browser) and Windows [REAL]
- Price: Free — the itch page shows no price and offers the download without a payment prompt [SECONDARY]
- Free browser/web demo: **Yes — the full game plays in the browser** ("Birdography Web.zip — Play in browser") [REAL]
- Store: https://notapsycho.itch.io/birdography
- What it is: photograph birds to unlock them in a journal and learn more about them; controls are right-mouse camera, left-mouse shutter, F journal [REAL]
- Design lesson for Keeper's Frame: the smallest possible photo loop still needs a journal — Birdography pairs every shot with an immediate, readable record (the journal entry), which is exactly the feedback Mara's arrangements and Iona's remembered act are missing when the result is only "felt".

## 3. Paper Trail — (b) diorama / scene manipulation

- Creator: Newfangled Games (Henry and Frederick Hoffman) [SECONDARY]
- Release: 21 May 2024 [REAL]
- Platforms: Steam (Windows, Mac), Nintendo Switch, PS4/PS5, Xbox One/Series X|S, mobile via Netflix Games [REAL]
- Price: $19.99 USD on Steam [REAL]
- Free browser/web demo: No. A Steam demo was live at launch and remained live after release [REAL]
- Store: Steam bundle page (verified the $19.99 price): https://store.steampowered.com/bundle/76257/Carto__Map_Map__Paper_Trail/ — the standalone app URL was not verified in this sweep, so it is not listed here.
- What it is: top-down puzzle adventure about leaving home, set in a foldable paper world; the player folds, spins and merges the world to join paths and solve puzzles, meeting a cast of characters along the way [REAL]
- Design lesson for Keeper's Frame: Paper Trail teaches one manipulation and then varies only the scene, never the verb — Keeper's Frame should likewise keep Iona's and Mara's single verbs (place / remember) constant and let the stories, not new controls, carry the novelty.

## 4. Tiny Glade — (b) diorama / scene manipulation

- Creator: Pounce Light (Anastasia Opara and Tomasz Stachowiak) [SECONDARY]
- Release: 23 September 2024 [REAL]
- Platforms: Steam (Windows, Linux at launch; macOS added 13 May 2026 [SECONDARY]); Steam Deck Verified [REAL]
- Price: $14.99 USD on Steam [REAL]
- Free browser/web demo: No. A free Steam demo ran from June 2024 and a PC demo remains downloadable from the store page [REAL]
- Store: https://store.steampowered.com/app/2198150/Tiny_Glade/
- What it is: a cozy, gridless diorama builder — walls, windows, paths and roofs adapt to each other as placed (a path through a wall becomes a door; adjacent windows merge); no goals, no fail state; includes a built-in photo mode with camera modes and a first-person view [REAL]
- Design lesson for Keeper's Frame: Tiny Glade proves arrangement feedback must be visible in the scene itself (a door appears because a path crossed a wall), not in a menu — Mara's placements should change the diorama in a way the player can see without being told.

## 5. Lost Records: Bloom & Rage — (c) remembered choice / return

- Creator: DON'T NOD (developer and publisher) [REAL]
- Release: Tape 1 ("Bloom") 18 February 2025; Tape 2 ("Rage") 15 April 2025 as a free update [REAL]
- Platforms: Steam (Windows, Mac, Linux; Steam Deck Verified), PS5, Xbox Series X|S [REAL]
- Price: $39.99 USD base on Steam; the store search at crawl time showed a 50% sale at $19.99 [REAL]
- Free browser/web demo: No; no Steam demo is listed on the store page as crawled [SECONDARY]
- Store: https://store.steampowered.com/app/1902960/ (app id from Steam data; price verified via Steam search text) [SECONDARY for the app URL]
- What it is: narrative adventure in two timelines — summer 1995 and 27 years later — filmed on a camcorder; four friends' choices in the past are re-examined in the present; roughly 10–12 hours across both tapes [REAL]
- Design lesson for Keeper's Frame: Lost Records makes the return visit the whole game by keeping the recording device (the camcorder) as the bridge between timelines — Keeper's Frame needs an equally concrete bridge object between Iona's first visit and her return, or the "remembered" payoff reads as a flag rather than a memory.

## 6. 1000xRESIST — (c) remembered choice / return

- Creator: sunset visitor (斜陽過客); publisher Fellow Traveller [REAL]
- Release: 9 May 2024 [REAL]
- Platforms: Steam (Windows, Mac, Linux; Steam Deck Playable) [REAL]
- Price: $19.99 USD base on Steam; 40% sale to $11.99 at crawl time [REAL]
- Free browser/web demo: No demo found in the crawled store text [OPEN]
- Store: https://store.steampowered.com/app/1675830/ (app id from Steam data) [SECONDARY for the app URL]
- What it is: sci-fi narrative adventure; the player is Watcher, serving the ALLMOTHER a thousand years after an alien invasion, and relives the past through "communions" that reframe everything the player thought they knew; multiple endings; 10–15 hours [REAL]
- Design lesson for Keeper's Frame: 1000xRESIST earns its revelations by letting the player revisit the same memory with new context and a different action available — the return visit must offer a new choice, not just a replay of the first visit with a different line of dialogue.
