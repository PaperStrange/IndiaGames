# Validated Practices — from 13 play sessions + professional cross-check
**Date:** 2026-10-15 · **Sequence:** study→execute→contrast→validate→practices
**Rule honored:** a practice appears here only if the insight survived contrast against both the original session beat and a named professional position. Session-only observations and the methodological boundary are excluded (see contrast doc).

---

1. **Earn the rug-pull.** Give the player a genuine felt win before any subversion — and make the subversion's purpose legible. Purpose-free "gotchas" read as trolling, not design. *(Portal cube, Wolpaw & Swift; Undertale timing rule; Miyamoto/Tezuka; Williams; PH2015 — subversion needs a reason)*
2. **Hide the true skill behind a decoy.** Teach an obvious skill; let the real skill reveal itself later, consistent with the world's own rules. *(Undertale EXP→"execution points," PH2015; Portal cube→GLaDOS; Braid consistency, Blow)*
3. **Make remembered choices playable, not announced.** Callbacks must be felt in performance, not declared in UI prompts — and expect the Telltale objection: if the plot never diverges, players will call it decorative. *(Mass Effect camp, Hudson/Norman; Scheubel — perform, don't announce; Telltale critics — the standing counter-position)*
4. **Quote the player back to themselves.** Reflect their own words or behavior — even across resets — and recognition does the emotional work. *(Undertale memory across resets; Deltarune player-name device; Stanley Parable's mirror)*
5. **Cut fast between tones.** Whiplash pivots — sincere setup, sudden cut — are the joke; calibrate the pause to the player's own behavior, and never repeat a gag into flatness. *(GDC'10 humor panel: Schafer, Vanaman; Goose Game set-pieces; South Park anticipation)*
6. **Train before you test.** Every mechanic gets a safe rehearsal before its real trial; fair failure means an immediate, readable consequence — never a mockery. *(Portal training; Mario 1-1; Swift on low-stress learning; PH2015 on fair failure)*
7. **Make complicity manual.** If you want the player to feel responsible, their own hands must do it — through familiar mechanics, with the consequence faced, not teleported past. Aim for reflection, not guilt-tripping; withhold judgment. *(Williams, Spec Ops — earned, key to narrative, non-judgmental)*
8. **Hold the state, free the thinking.** Externalize bookkeeping (journals, note panels) so the player's working memory stays on deduction, not admin. *(Pope, Obra Dinn book; Beachum, Outer Wilds ship log)*
9. **Make every aha checkable.** The click must be recognition of a real idea, retraceable against the world's consistent laws — never a trick that makes the player feel smart. *(Blow, anti-trickery; PH2015 — logical, player-brought insight)*
10. **Price choices immediately.** Binary decisions land when their costs show at once; don't judge the player's morals for them. *(Wreden — immediacy; Williams — no judgment; PH2015 — no "proper way")*
11. **Let player artifacts be the social.** Persistent traces of other players don't substitute for the social experience — they are it. Design them as such. *(Kojima, Death Stranding; Miyazaki, Dark Souls messages)*
12. **Let players linger; don't meter coziness.** Design rooms worth staying in after their purpose is done; completion percentages and extrinsic rewards work against the feeling. *(PH2017 Designing for Coziness — safety/abundance/softness; parking prescribed)*

## Explicitly NOT practices (kept honest)
- **Punish detachment with flatness** (PROMPTBOX loop 1) — one session, no professional source found. Hypothesis, not practice.
- **Legible walls beat ambiguous stalls** (Dark Room vs. Different Strokes) — one session, genuine gap in the professional literature. Hypothesis, not practice.
- **Withhold judgment on unobservable loops** (Make it Meme) — a methodological boundary, not a design claim. It stays a rule for the reporter, not a practice for the designer.
