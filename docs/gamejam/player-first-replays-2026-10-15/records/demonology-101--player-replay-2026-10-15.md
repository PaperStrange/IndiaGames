## What actually happened

**23:41–23:43** — Opened the page, clicked Run game. Godot boot, language select (ENGLISH / ESPAÑOL). Chose ENGLISH. The game lives in a retro-window UI: left column buttons (RESET GAME, FULLSCREEN, SUMMON, TALK, CONFIRM NAME), right side the "necronomicon.org" catalog ("demons 0/5"), page 1/18. **23:44–00:05** — Read all 18 catalog pages before summoning anything. This was the right call and also the slow part — pages flip one at a time, no jump. Highlights that stuck: page 3, the rules ("Demons tell the truth unless their page says otherwise," "Enslave 5 demons to make your wish come true"); page 4, "Obvious, captain of the void" (overloads you with obvious info; a Wikipedia contributor — very funny); page 9, "Bill, just Bill" (the funniest page: super successful, everyone loves Bill, incredibly humble — the catalog's author praising himself in the third person; I laughed out loud); page 10, "eeleeth, dreead of heell" (doubles every E); page 13, "aargh, destroyer of pizza" (older of two siblings, always tells the truth); page 18, "eergh, first dawn witness" (younger of two siblings, "the opposite of their older sibling").

**00:06** — Clicked SUMMON. Demon 1: a black sun-like creature with wavy rays, oval body with a vertical slit, two stubby legs, standing on the pentagram. Cute in a WigglyPaint way.

**DEMON 1 — bbaddon, queen of dance.** TALK gave 5 cycling lines: "AM I THE FIRST DEMON YOU SUMMON? THAT'S SO SWEET" / "YOU PROBABLY THOUGHT THIS WAS GOING TO BE PIECE OF CAKE, RIGHT?" / "MY NAME IS NOT BILL, BY THE WAY. JUST SO YOU KNOW." / "HEY, I WON'T SUGARCOAT IT. BEING A DEMONOLOGIST IS HARD" / "YOU'RE SO CLUELESS. THIS IS LIKE TAKING CANDY FROM A BABY."
Four of five lines contain sugar references (SWEET, CAKE, SUGARCOAT, CANDY). Page 6: "bbaddon, queen of dance — Can't stop talking about sugary things." The click: the demon literally cannot stop talking about sugary things. No other page fits (the coffee-without-sugar demons don't match; eeleeth would double every E, which the dialogue doesn't). Navigated back 12 pages to page 6, hit CONFIRM NAME.
How it landed: TRIUMPH. The demon vanished; the catalog ticked to "demons 1/5." A genuine rush — the clue snapped together like a pun clicking into place. And "MY NAME IS NOT BILL" is a true statement (demons tell the truth) — a cute nod to the catalog's self-mythologizing author.

**DEMON 2 — eergh, first dawn witness.** SUMMON: a one-eyed black creature with long thin limbs. TALK: "HEY, I WAS ABOUT TO HAVE A CUP OF COFFEE!" / "YEAH, I WAS UP LATE AT NIGHT WATCHING THAT TENNIS MATCH" / "I INVITED MY BROTHER OVER BECAUSE HE'S A BIG FAN TOO" / "AND SUCH A BIG LIER, LIKE NEVER TRUST THAT GUY" (sic — "LIER").
The click: this is the logic puzzle. "Brother" → the sibling pair (aargh, older, always tells the truth; eergh, younger, "the opposite of their older sibling" → always lies). If this demon is eergh the liar, every line inverts into its catalog facts: "coffee" (lie) → "Will refuse any drink other than water" ✓; "UP LATE at night" (lie) → "Goes to bed early to see the dawn" ✓; "brother is a big LIER, NEVER TRUST THAT GUY" (lie) → the brother tells the truth and SHOULD be trusted → aargh ✓. The tennis was flavor for the "up late" lie. Navigated forward 12 pages to page 18, hit CONFIRM NAME.
How it landed: TRIUMPH. "demons 2/5." This one felt even better than the first — not pattern-matching but a real deduction, flipping each statement through the opposite mechanic. I felt clever in exactly the way the game designed for. (Also: the "LIER" typo — noticed, possibly a bug, possibly a very committed bit.)

**~00:15** — Stopped after the second confirm. Two for two, never mocked — which almost feels like missing part of the game, but I'll take it.

## What kind of fun this was
A comedy logic-puzzle. The first two demons teach the two core skills: (1) match speech patterns to catalog quirks, (2) invert statements through the truth/lie mechanics. The writing is the star — Bill's self-aggrandizing page, the demon denying it's Bill, "Obvious, captain of the void" the Wikipedia contributor. The "click" of a correct name is a little jolt of "oh, OF COURSE" — for bbaddon a pun snapping shut, for eergh a logic gate flipping. Friction, honestly: the catalog flips one page at a time with no jump, so each confirm meant 12 clicks through a tiny Godot canvas, and the itch page kept dumping spoiler-filled comments into view while scrolling. The game is delightful; the page-turning is a slog.
