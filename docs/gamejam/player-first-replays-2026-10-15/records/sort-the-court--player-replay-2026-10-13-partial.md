## My reign, as I lived it
Day 1: A little blue creature in a party hat asked "Hiya! Do you like my party hat?" — of course I said yes. An evangelist lady asked for "a second to talk about our lord and saviour" — declined. A Knight asked to "embark upon a treacherous quest" — yes — and rode off with "For the kingdom! I shall return to you, lord!"

Day 2: Hired Sneaky Girl — "steal from the rich and give to... you? Not just the rich, I'll steal from whoever, basically." Gold +50, happiness -5. Then Winston, a green slime: "Hey uhhhh, you think you could tell people to stoooop callin' me 'slimeball'? It's hurtin' my feeelings..." — YES, and he melted with "Thaaaanks boooosssssss..." (+3 happiness). Favorite moment: decreeing an end to slime-bullying from the throne. Then a random event: "Success! -50 to happiness +500 to gold" — gold rocketed to 750, happiness cratered to 48. Never fully understood what "succeeded," which annoyed me.

Then Chester, a treasure-chest mimic: "I'm hungry. Can I eat some folks? I've got plenty of gold, so I can pay you back for their... lives..." — NO, twice across two days ("Ah, fine, I'll leave them alone."). And then the gut punch: he came back with "I accidentally ate some folks out front, can I just pay you back with gold?" HE DID IT ANYWAY. Took the blood money (+150 gold, -5 pop) and felt genuinely rotten. Worst moment of the run.

The Witch tried to shake me down for 200 gold as an "offering" — refused; the spirits were already appeased. Georgie the demon offered a coin flip: "Heads, I give you a buncha gold. Tails, I take a bunch of yer people's souls." Hard no. He came back wanting to buy my people's happiness — no again. ("Ehh, I'll getcha next time, buddy.")

Then the DRAGON: "Booo! Give me your gold or I'll eat your townspeople!" I paid — 400 gold gone in one swoop (403 → 3), dragon stomping off with "Haha, yes! I'll take that!" Treasury empty, felt sick, but my people were safe. The decision that hurt most. The Blacksmith offered to forge a dragon-slaying blade if I funded his search — I had 3 gold, had to decline ("I'm disapppointed, but at the same time I understand.").

Rebuilding was the most fun stretch: welcomed a Blacksmith's shop (+3 pop), patted Boots the cat's belly ("Mmmrrrrow."), let a visiting Chicken be ("Bok. (It left behind an egg!!" — +3 gold, rent paid in eggs!), gave Grandma a coin for her newspaper ("Thank you dearie." — down to 2 gold, worth it), gazed into Lil Fang's vampire crystal (+50 gold, +5 happiness), laughed at Skelly's "Do you find me humerus?" pun (+2 happiness, needed that after Chester), welcomed refugees (+15 pop), formed a military alliance (+15 pop), sold construction supplies to clear a -23 gold debt (watching my little town visually shrink back to huts stung), kept the Sneaky Girl's confiscated loot for the crown (+25 gold), and twice communed with the All-Seeing Eye: first it rained +100 gold on me, then it took 100 back ("I feel... unlucky!"). Never trust a giant eyeball twice.

The run ended not with a bang but with a dead screen. Never got my finale — no glorious victory, no tragic downfall, just a curtain falling mid-scene. That felt genuinely bad; I was invested.

## What kind of fun this was
The fun of being handed a crown and immediately discovering that every good instinct costs you something. Saying yes feels great for about ten seconds — the slime's joy, the cat's purr, the refugees arriving — and then the dragon eats your treasury or your pet chest-monster eats your taxpayers. A comedy of consequences: the game keeps asking "what kind of king are you?" and my honest answer by day 6 was "a soft-hearted one who is terrible with money." I laughed a lot, I winced a lot, and I genuinely cared whether my little pixel people were happy. Losing the run to a technical failure instead of a royal downfall is my one real grievance — I wanted to see how my story ended.
