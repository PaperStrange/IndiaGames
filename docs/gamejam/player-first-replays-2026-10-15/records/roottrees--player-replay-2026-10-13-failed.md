## What kind of fun this was
Not the deduction fun — never got there. The atmosphere fun, in fragments: a bleak newspaper headline, a woman in a doorway mourning girls who died too young, a lawyer talking blood relatives and money, an empty family tree waiting on a wall. This is a game about patience and attention — reading obituaries, squinting at photos, feeling a name lock into place. I re-felt the promise of that, the mood of it, but not the thing itself. Like being shown the cover of a mystery novel and then having it taken away. The fun I remember — the slow, obsessive, deeply satisfying click of a correct name — stayed a memory this time. I wanted it badly, and I couldn't reach it, and that wanting is the most honest thing I can report.
