## 1. Earn the rug-pull — ALIGNED*
- **Original beat:** PROMPTBOX loop 2 — palmed the knife, VELLO searched the empty shelf, gasped, she died anyway. "It let me feel brilliant for exactly long enough."
- **Pro:** Portal companion cube (Wolpaw & Swift, GDC'08) — gift → forced destruction, punishment doubles as training. Undertale timing rule (PH2015) — bond must be taught first. Mario power-up then loss. Williams — every cruelty "had to be earned." PH2015 — subversion needs specific purpose; "Oops! Gotcha!" = failure.
- **Fresh-session nuance:** the re-shoot found palming the knife alone does NOT stop the murder ("NOTHING NEW TONIGHT") — the "win" was illusory even mechanically. This strengthens the reading (the game let the player feel brilliant while the outcome was fixed) but the insight must not imply the win was mechanically real.
- **Verdict:** ALIGNED*. Fix: clarify the win was felt, not mechanical.
