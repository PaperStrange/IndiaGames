Fresh live-browser replays of the 13 competitor slots, played **as a player, not a designer**: what surprised, what was funny, what moved or frustrated, and exactly how far each session got. No walkthroughs, hints, or fabricated completion — unfinished sessions are bounded and guesses labeled.

## Contents

- `player-first-report-2026-10-15.md` — the player-first report (source for the hosted page "Player-First Play Report"; plural-reviewed before build).
- `records/` — the raw per-session player records, 18 files:
  - 13 slots: Temple of No, Adventures With Anxiety!, PROMPTBOX, Make it Meme, A Dark Room, The Red Pearls of Borneo, There Is No Game, Detective: Again!, Sort the Court!, We Become What We Behold, Different Strokes, Six Cats Under (parked at the kitchen partial per Sakura), Demonology 101 (verified browser replacement for Roottrees).
  - Audit trail: the two failed Roottrees attempts, the failed Six Cats first attempt, the Golden Idol replacement attempt that failed (official browser demo gone), and the Demonology 101 access check.
- `snapshots/` — the 4 surviving screenshots (WBWWB ×3, Demonology 101 ×1); most captures died in tooling, as the report notes.

## Perspective separation

This package is the **player** perspective. Game-design insights drawn from the same sessions, if any, are recorded separately as the **designer** perspective — never mixed into this report.
